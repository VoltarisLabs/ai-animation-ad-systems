## DigitalMarketer

### Customer Value Journey (Ryan Deiss): 8 stages
1. **Aware**: they discover you.
2. **Engage**: they consume your content.
3. **Subscribe**: they give contact info (a lead magnet).
4. **Convert**: they make a small first commitment. This is an "entry-point offer" that asks little of them, a low-price purchase or a free consult.
5. **Excite**: the first purchase delivers a quick win.
6. **Ascend**: they buy the core offer, then more.
7. **Advocate**: they give reviews and testimonials.
8. **Promote**: they refer others.

### Before and After Grid (Deiss)
Map what the buyer **has, feels, their average day, and their status** before and after. Ad copy lives in the gap between the two.

### The Ad Grid (Molly Pittman)
Match messages to markets in a grid: avatars down one side, angles across the top. Test each cell as its own ad. This is meant to end "one hit wonder" ads.

### UPSYD (Keith Krance, Perpetual Traffic)
Target ads by awareness: **U**naware, **P**roblem-aware, **S**olution-aware, **Y**our-solution aware, **D**eal-aware. It adapts Schwartz's awareness levels for paid traffic.

### Video ad script template (industry standard)
**Hook -> Problem -> Agitate -> Solution -> CTA.** This is PAS with a thumb-stopping hook in the first 1 to 3 seconds.

**Rules**
- Never ask cold traffic for the big purchase. Offer a low-risk entry-point offer first.
- Build one ad per avatar-angle cell. Kill losers and scale winners.
- Match the ad to the awareness stage (UPSYD).

**Triggers:** Low-risk first commitments (foot in the door), excitement right after purchase, before/after contrast.
