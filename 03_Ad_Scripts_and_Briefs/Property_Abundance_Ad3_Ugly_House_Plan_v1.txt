PART 3 – CHECK EACH CLIP BEFORE YOU KEEP IT
============================================================
Regenerate the clip if any of these happen:
[ ] Any object jumps, slides or changes place by itself
[ ] Any readable or garbled text on paper, boxes, signs or screens
[ ] Skin looks smooth, waxy or airbrushed
[ ] Light looks glowy and golden like a movie
[ ] Hands have the wrong number of fingers or bend strangely
[ ] The buyer's face, hair or clothes change between clips
[ ] The mood looks sad or heavy. It should feel light and fun.
If the buyer changes: take a screenshot of his best frame from CLIP 1 and attach it to the prompts for CLIP 2, 3 and 4 as the reference image. Do the same with the seller from CLIP 3 for CLIP 4.

PART 4 – ASSEMBLE IN CAPCUT
============================================================
1. Put the 4 clips in order. Trim each shot to match the script timing in PART 1.
2. Add an upbeat, light music track with no lyrics. Put the cuts on the beat.
3. Record or generate the voiceover from PART 1 with an upbeat, friendly voice. Music at about 15 percent under the voice. Gemini sound at about 10 percent.
4. Captions: big, bold, 1 to 4 words per card, one keyword in color (use yellow for STILL BUYING), in the top third. Keep clear of the bottom 20 percent and the right edge.
5. At 28-31s, pop in NO REPAIRS / NO CLEANING / NO SHOWINGS one line at a time.
6. Last 2 seconds: place your real form screenshot on the white phone screen from CLIP 4 and add the Property Abundance logo.
7. Export 1080x1920, 30 fps.
