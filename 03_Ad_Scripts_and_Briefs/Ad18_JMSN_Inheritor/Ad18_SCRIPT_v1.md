## 1. Brief (checklist Steps 0-2, which the process keeps)

```
Product/offer:      Property Abundance buys the house itself, as it is. Belongings can stay. No commission,
                    no fees or closing costs, the seller picks the closing date, no obligation.
Audience:           An adult child who inherited a parent's house, often living hours away.
Their #1 pain:      "I don't want to clean this entire place out."            (README, Representative Voice)
Their #1 want:      "I just want to be done with it."                          (README, Representative Voice)

Proof we have:      None yet. No sale, no reviews. propertyabundanceusa.com does not load (see section 11).
Offer + first step: Tap the button below, answer a few questions, get an offer. No obligation.
```

## 5. Loop sequence

`OPEN A (L1, 0.00 s) → OPEN B (L2, 2.73 s) → CLOSE A (L4, 9.20 s) → CLOSE B (L13, 23.86 s)`

This is staggered with 2 loops.

**A: "Don't clean out your parents' house just yet."**
- The open question: why not, and what then?
- It closes on L4, "Their things? Leave them right there.", while B is still open.
- L2 and L3 sit between A's open and close, so it never opens and closes back to back.

**B: "...and you'll finally be done with it."**
- The promise: when is it over?
- It closes on L13, "That's the day you're done.", straight after "You pick the closing date."
- This is the last close, and it carries the biggest payoff: the persona's core desire, closure (README, "From unwanted responsibility → closure").

**The offer isn't hidden.** "Buy" lands at 3.07 s and the deal is complete at 11.25 s. B carries a promise, not the offer (brief).

**Evidence:**
- Keeping B open across the no-list and CTA 1 is the "payload inside the loop" case: material shown during an open curiosity gap was remembered better (Gruber 2014, psychology report §4b and §4e).
- The loop order itself has no head-to-head test (§4e).

**One term redefined** (reel_direction_2): "done" = the closing date you pick.
