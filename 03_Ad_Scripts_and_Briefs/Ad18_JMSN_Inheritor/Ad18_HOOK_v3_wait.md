# Ad 18 hook v3: "Wait. You can sell your house without paying a realtor."

Written 2026-09-28, after the user's note: "the starting hook starts with something interesting, not an introduction: something interesting and something very profitable to the customers." It is built from all five references, including R5 (`ref_1.mp4`, `04_Audience_Research/Ref_Ads_Research.md` §11).

## What every reference hook does in its first seconds
| Ref | First words | The interesting part | What the customer gets | Where the intro comes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | "Don't sell your house without getting bonus cash." | a warning | free cash | never |
| R2 | "Why are you still paying the mortgage for a house you want to get rid of?" | a question that calls them out | stop paying | never |
| R3 | "Send me your address and I'll show you what I can offer…" | a direct ask | a number for their house | never |
| R4 | "This house sold for cash in eight days." | proof you can see | cash, fast | never |
| R5 | "Wait, I can buy your house. Any condition, at any price…" | "Wait" + palm to the lens | a buyer, whatever the condition | 4.88 s, after the whole offer |

None of the five opens with a name or a company. Each one opens with something that stops you, and money or ease for the viewer.

Most of the references' "profit" is speed or a bonus, and we can't claim either. Our honest profit is what the seller **doesn't pay**:
- no commission (no realtor);
- as-is (no contractor);
- belongings can stay (no clean-out crew).

## The hook
```text
[OPEN A]   Wait. You can sell your house without paying a realtor.
           SHOW → JMSN, close front-camera selfie, eye level. On "Wait." he raises his open palm to the lens (R5's stop gesture), then lowers it and leans in. No cut. Top banner on every frame: SELL IT AS IT IS · NO COMMISSION | CAP: WAIT. / YOU CAN *SELL* / YOUR HOUSE / WITHOUT PAYING / A *REALTOR*
[payload]  Or a contractor. Or a cleaning crew.
           SHOW → "Or a contractor.": NEEDS CLIP, a tool belt and a drill on a torn-up floor (objects only), red X. "Or a cleaning crew.": NEEDS CLIP, a mop, a bucket and full trash bags (objects only), red X. | CAP: OR A *CONTRACTOR* / OR A *CLEANING CREW*
[OPEN B]   Here's what I'll do.
           SHOW → back to JMSN, a small nod. | CAP: HERE'S WHAT *I'LL DO*
[payload]  I'll buy it exactly as it is.
           SHOW → same shot. | CAP: I'LL *BUY* IT / EXACTLY *AS IT IS*
```
- **Timing at JMSN's 176 wpm:**
  - "sell" at 1.02 s;
  - the first sentence ends at 3.41 s, which is the first cut (R5 holds the face until 4.91 s);
  - "buy" at 7.50 s;
  - the 4 lines take 9.55 s.
- **At R5's 235 wpm:** "sell" at 0.77 s, the first sentence ends at 2.55 s.
- **The matching close** later in the script: `[CLOSE A] No realtor. No contractor. No cleaning crew. You pay none of them.` B closes on "That's the deal." after the deal, as in Script A.

**Why it works:**
- **"Wait" + the palm** is a command to stop, plus sudden motion toward the lens (orienting response, Ref_Ads_Research [6]).
- **"You can…"** offers a possibility most sellers haven't considered.
- **Three people they'd normally pay,** each a concrete cost, and each gets its own picture.
- **The claims are true to our confirmed terms:** no commission, as-is, belongings can stay.
- **No introduction.** The brand appears only on the banner and the end card.

**Backup hook (R5's exact order, the offer first):** "Wait. I'll buy your house as it is. You pay no commission, no fees, and no closing costs." It is 18 words. "buy" lands at 0.68 s and the first sentence ends at 2.73 s (176 wpm).

**Not used:** "keep the commission" and "costs you nothing". Both suggest the seller ends up with more money, and our offer vs. a retail sale isn't confirmed.
