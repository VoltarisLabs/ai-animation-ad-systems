## 2. R1 line by line, and what changed

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't sell your house without getting bonus cash. | Don't pay a dime to sell your house. | There is no bonus. Our profit is what the seller doesn't pay. Same length (8 words), same "Don't…" warning. |

| 6 | If this sounds like what you've been looking for, I'd love to give you a no obligation cash offer. | If that's what you need, let me make you a cash offer. No obligation. | Reworded and shortened so clip 4 fits 10 s. |
| 7 | Just tap the button right below this video to get started. | Just tap the button under this video to start. | Reworded. |
| 8 | All you'll need to do is answer a few simple questions about your property and we'll send your custom offer within 24 hours. | All you do is answer a few quick questions about the house, and I'll work up your offer. | "Within 24 hours" isn't confirmed. |
| 9 | We make the whole process quick, clear, and stress free. | And you pick the closing date. Your calendar, not mine. | "Quick" is a speed claim. The seller picks the closing date (confirmed). |

| 12 | Hit the button below. | Hit that button down below. | Reworded. |
| 13 | Tell us a little about your home and find out what your offer looks like today. | Tell me about the house and see what I'd pay for it. | "Today" reads as a speed claim. |

Shared words with R1, per line: 2 to 11 (lines 3, 4, 9, 11 share 2 each; line 8 shares 11 of 18). The order, the beats and the rhythm are R1's; the wording is ours.

**If Property Abundance really pays a perk** (moving costs, or a cash bonus), R1's own hook can come back: "Don't sell your house without getting your move paid for." Only with the user's confirmation that the perk is real.

## 5. Watch-outs

- **"Don't pay a dime"** rests on the confirmed no commission, no fees and no closing costs, plus as-is and belongings can stay. If any cost can land on the seller at closing (for example a transfer tax a state puts on the seller by law), the hook must change.
- **"Answer a few quick questions"** needs a form that asks them. propertyabundanceusa.com still fails (403 over http, a certificate error over https, measured 2026-09-27).
- **Pace:** 46.36 s at 176 wpm is 3.48 s longer than R1. Ask Omni for a brisk, energetic delivery.
