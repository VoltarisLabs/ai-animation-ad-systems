# Ad 18 v3 "The Ugliest House": script + visual plan

Written 2026-09-28, after the user's feedback on the v3 hook: "Still not interesting enough … give me the script, and then give me a plan for the visuals" so it can be generated with AI.
- **Evidence:** `04_Audience_Research/Ref_Ads_Research.md` (R1-R4 §1-10, R5 §11).
- **Prompts:** `10_Video_Builds/Ad18_JMSN_Inheritor/Ad18_VISUAL_PLAN_v3_prompts.md`.
- **Status:** nothing generated, no paid API called.

## Why this hook
The earlier hooks were claims. The references work because something happens in the first seconds:
- R5 stops you with a palm to the lens;
- R4 drives past a real house;
- R2 and R3 open on hands at work.

**v3's hook is a 6-second scene with a reveal.** JMSN is out hunting for the ugliest house on the street, rejects two nice ones, and picks the wreck.
- **Interesting:** a buyer who *wants* the worst house flips what a seller expects. The two "Not that one" beats build anticipation, and "That one." pays it off.
- **Profitable:** "pay cash" is heard at 1.02 s. The viewer's biggest problem, the state of the house, becomes the reason someone wants it.
- **No introduction.** The brand appears only on the end card.

## Part 1. The script

107 words, 36.48 s at JMSN's measured 176 wpm. Every time below is word count × pace.

```text
[OPEN A]   I'm here to pay cash for the ugliest house on this street.
           SHOW → 0.00-4.09 s. JMSN, arm's-length selfie, walking a neat suburban sidewalk, looking left and right at the houses as he talks. | BANNER (whole ad): SELL IT AS IT IS · NO COMMISSION | CAP: I'M HERE TO / PAY *CASH* / FOR THE *UGLIEST* / HOUSE ON THIS STREET
[payload]  Not that one. Not that one.
           SHOW → 4.09-5.11 s: neat house A, a quick handheld whip-pan that lands on it. 5.11-6.14 s: neat house B, a whip-pan the other way. | CAP: NOT *THAT* ONE / NOT *THAT* ONE
[OPEN B]   That one.
           SHOW → 6.14-8.18 s: THE wreck, a whip-pan that lands, then a slow step toward the fence. It holds through "Holes in the roof." | CAP: *THAT* ONE.
[payload]  Holes in the roof. Yard's a jungle. I'll buy it exactly like this.
           SHOW → "Holes in the roof" stays on the wreck (the tarp over the roof hole). 8.18 s: JMSN in the wreck's front yard, knee-high weeds, looks down on "jungle", back to the lens and a nod on "exactly like this". | CAP: *HOLES* IN THE ROOF / YARD'S A *JUNGLE* / I'LL *BUY* IT / EXACTLY LIKE THIS
[CLOSE B]  The worse it looks, the more work you skip.
           SHOW → same shot, a small grin. | CAP: THE *WORSE* IT LOOKS / THE MORE WORK / YOU *SKIP*
[payload]  You don't fix it. You don't clean it. You don't even empty it.
           SHOW → 14.32-15.68 s: close push-in on a fist-sized hole in the drywall (no people). 15.68 s: JMSN inside the wreck's living room, full of left-behind furniture and boxes; he sweeps his free hand around the room on "empty it". | CAP: YOU DON'T *FIX* IT / YOU DON'T *CLEAN* IT / YOU DON'T EVEN / *EMPTY* IT
[payload]  No realtor. No commission. No fees. No closing costs.
           SHOW → 18.75-20.11 s: forsale_7578271 from 8.0 s (plain FOR SALE sign, red X added in the edit). 20.11-21.82 s: contract_7841865 (contract and pens, no hands). | CAP: NO *REALTOR* / NO *COMMISSION* / NO *FEES* / NO CLOSING *COSTS*
[CTA 1]    Tap below. Tell me where it is.
           SHOW → 21.82 s: JMSN on the wreck's concrete stoop by the crooked screen door (new location), points down past the bottom of the frame on "Tap below". | CAP: *TAP* BELOW / TELL ME *WHERE* IT IS
[payload]  You pick the closing date. No obligation. Don't like my number? Walk away.
           SHOW → same shot to 28.64 s; open palms on "No obligation", a small shrug on "Walk away". | CAP: YOU *PICK* / THE CLOSING DATE / NO *OBLIGATION* / DON'T LIKE MY *NUMBER*? / *WALK AWAY*
[payload]  I buy it myself. No middleman.
           SHOW → 28.64 s: JMSN back in the front yard (callback to 8.18 s), hand to chest on "myself" (trust line, no cutaway). | CAP: I BUY IT *MYSELF* / NO *MIDDLEMAN*
[CLOSE A]  If yours is the ugly one on the street, good. That's the one I want.
           SHOW → same shot; thumb over his shoulder at the house on "the ugly one", a grin on "good". | CAP: IF YOURS IS / THE *UGLY* ONE / ON THE STREET, *GOOD* / THAT'S THE ONE / I *WANT*
[CTA 2]    Tap below.
           SHOW → points down, then the logo end card. | CAP: *TAP* BELOW
```

**Loop sequence:** OPEN A 0.00 s → OPEN B 6.14 s → CLOSE B 11.25 s → CLOSE A 30.68 s. It is nested, and it never opens and closes back to back. A closes last, on the hook's own promise.

**Checks** (from `11_Psychological_Hooks/OFFER_FIRST_PROCESS.md`, plus the money-stake rule):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Money at stake + "you" + the house in the hook | PASS: "pay cash", "the ugliest house", and the "yours" payoff at 30.68 s |
| Offer word by 4.5 s | PASS: "pay" 1.02 s, "cash" 1.36 s |
| Hook 3 s or less | FAIL by 1.09 s: the first sentence takes 4.09 s. R5 also holds its hook on the face for 4.91 s |
| First cut at 2.5-2.8 s | FAIL: 4.09 s, for the same reason. At a brisk 200 wpm it lands at 3.60 s |
| Deal by about 13 s | PASS: "I'll buy it exactly like this" by 11.25 s |
| Confirmed terms | 7 of 7: cash buyer, as-is, belongings stay, no commission, no fees/closing costs, the seller picks the date, no obligation, buys it itself |
| CTA 1 at 30-60%, CTA 2 at the end | PASS: 59.8% and 35.80 s |
| No speed, bonus, scarcity or result claim | PASS |
| Loop order | PASS: nested A-B-B-A |
| Cuts | 10 in 36.48 s = 2.74 per 10 s, every one at a sentence start except inside the skit ("Not that one." ×2, "That one."), which are sentence starts too |
| Only JMSN on screen | PASS: every other shot is a house, a room or an object |

**Watch-outs:**
- **Trademark (checked 2026-09-28).** HomeVestors of America owns the registered marks "WE BUY UGLY HOUSES" and "WE BUY UGLY HOUSES.COM" (Reg. No. 5560021), and it sues competitors who use them.
  - Sources: [Justia Trademarks](https://trademarks.justia.com/877/67/we-buy-ugly-houses-87767091.html), [uspto.report](https://uspto.report/TM/76556516), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/homevestors-files-suit-against-trademark-infringers-162774136.html), [Business Wire 2021](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20210222005298/en/HomeVestors-Settles-Lawsuit-Against-Serial-Trademark-Infringer).
  - v3 uses "ugly" only to describe the viewer's house ("the ugliest house on this street", "the ugly one"). It never says "we buy ugly houses" or anything close to it.
  - Keep "ugly" out of the banner, the landing page, any domain and the ad's headline. Get a quick legal check before spend, because HomeVestors enforces the mark.
- **Tone.** "If yours is the ugly one… good" is playful and doesn't accuse. If a test shows it lands badly, the softer twin is "the house on this street nobody else wants".
- **The form.** "Tell me where it is" means the form must ask for the address first. propertyabundanceusa.com still fails (403 over http, a certificate error over https).

## Part 2. What visuals to use (from the 5 references)

| Visual device | What the references do (measured) | What Ad 18 v3 uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame 1 moves and gives you something to look at** | R5: a palm to the lens. R4: a drive-by of a house. R2, R3: hands at work. R1: a bed of cash | JMSN walking a street, scanning the houses: the hunt |
| **Presenter selfie** at arm's length, eye level | R1 walking, 63.5% face time. R5 standing, 70.9% face time, **4 locations** | JMSN selfie in 4 places: street → the wreck's yard → inside → stoop → yard again |
| **A picture for each thing named** | R2-R4: the median shot carries 2-8 words | Neat house A, neat house B, the wreck, the hole, the FOR SALE sign, the contract |
| **Before vs after inside the ad** | Condition shots vs SOLD and clean houses | Neat houses vs the wreck, inside the skit itself |
| **Faces reacting on the key lines** | 5 per montage (R2-R4) | JMSN's own reactions (head shakes, the grin, the point). He is the only person, per the b-roll rule |
| **Phone-shot realism** | Handheld, natural light, real cluttered rooms | Overcast daylight, handheld, deep focus, real skin, weeds and clutter (`veo-realism-prompt-rules`) |
| **Text on screen** | Captions on every line (all 5). R5: a permanent top banner | 2-4 word captions with one coloured keyword, plus the banner "SELL IT AS IT IS · NO COMMISSION", both at y = 269-1248 |
| **Edit rhythm** | Presenter ads: 1.40 (R1) and 2.67 (R5) edits per 10 s. Montage: 4.12-7.71 | 2.74 per 10 s, with fast whip-pans only in the skit |

1. Make still #1 first. The user approves it: it fixes the house for every later shot.

3. After the user approves the stills, make clips C1-C5 and H1-H4.
4. Word-diff every talking clip against the script. Omni has dropped and repeated phrases before.
5. **Airtable:** create Ad 18's row first. Attach the stills to Image 1/2 and the clips to Generated Videos.
