## Part B. Script breakdown

| # | Beat | Spoken line | Job of this line | Source | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Open A** (hook) | "Sell this house without fixing a thing." | Stop the scroll with a benefit, and open "how?" | Bullseye desire; Sabri's benefit-led rule | Curiosity + benefit |
| 2 | Payload | "Roof's going. Floor's soft. And that repair quote? Read twice. Folded in a drawer." | Turn her pain into scenes she has lived | Pain quotes, dimensionalized (Georgi) | Dimensionalization |
| 3 | **Open B** | "You're thinking, they'll offer pennies because it's ugly." | Say her objection before she scrolls away with it | Persona 4 objection | Unspoken sentence |
| 4 | Payload | "List it as-is instead? Buyers still inspect, bring a repair list, and haggle." | Name the alternative she's weighing, and its real cost | Research doc, alternatives table (Sabri: name the alternative) | Contrast |
| 5 | **Close B** | "So here's the honest part. Our offer is lower than a fixed-up sale." | Answer the objection by admitting the catch first | Approved self-incrimination line (Ad 15) | Damaging admission |
| 6 | Payload | "But you fix nothing. No fees. Leave the furniture. You pick the closing date." | Stack the confirmed terms as relief | Confirmed terms | Risk reversal |
| 7 | **Close A** | "That's why you sell it without fixing a thing. Property Abundance." | Pay off the hook, brand last | Loop rule: close A last, brand on the close | Consistency |
| 8 | CTA | "Tap Learn More. No obligation." | Smallest honest step | Sabri's soft CTA; confirmed "no obligation" | Zero-price first step |

**Loops:** Open A (1) → payload → Open B (3) → payload → Close B (5) → payload → Close A (7) → CTA. They close in reverse order, with a payload between each step.

**Length:** 85 words, about 30.7 s at 166 wpm (the measured Omni pace for Emily; measure JMSN after the first take).
