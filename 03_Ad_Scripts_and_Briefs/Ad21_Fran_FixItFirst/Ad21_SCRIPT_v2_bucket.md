## The set (one location for the whole ad)
A real, lived-in living room in an older house, turned into a podcast corner: two broadcast mics on boom arms, a small round wooden table, two mismatched armchairs, a floor lamp, a bookshelf, warm afternoon window light. Above them, a brown water stain on the ceiling. Between the chairs, a plastic bucket half full of water catches a slow drip. No sign and no branding on the set.

## The script
```text
[OPEN A]   AVA: Cash for your house. The bucket stays.
           SHOW → 0.0 s, first frame: macro of one drop falling into a half-full plastic bucket on
                  worn floorboards. PLINK. Rings spread. TEXT: "THE BUCKET STAYS".
                  0.8 s: wide shot of the set (the only wide until CLOSE A): AVA and JMSN at the

           SHOW → JMSN single, deadpan, on "So does the stain"; then a tilt up to the brown stain

           SHOW → AVA single. She glances up at the stain, then back at him, quieter, arms

[payload]  JMSN: Then someone says: fix it first. So you get a quote. You read it twice. Into the
                 drawer.
           SHOW → Black and white from here. AVA at a kitchen counter, under his voice: she reads

                  bumps the drawer with her hip. It won't fully shut.

[OPEN C]   JMSN: There's one reason "fix it first" never fit this house.
           SHOW → Back at the table, still black and white. JMSN single, looking around the room
                  as he says "this house". TEXT: "one reason"

[payload]  AVA: Let me guess. The roof.
           JMSN: Not the roof.
           SHOW → AVA single, a dry guess, eyes up at the ceiling. PLINK right after "roof".
                  Cut to JMSN single, a small smile, shaking his head.

                 fix it first, with your money, before anyone pays you.
           SHOW → JMSN single on "That one reason?". Then the villain as an object, black and
                  white: a miniature model of this house sits on the table between the mics; a
                  long paper receipt unrolls out of its tiny front door, over the table edge,
                  down to the floor, and keeps coming. TEXT: "fix it first" → "your money first"

[payload]  AVA: So what's different here?
           JMSN: Nobody here needs to fall in love. The sale is as-is. Bucket and all.
           SHOW → Colour comes back on AVA's question. The same miniature, same framing: the
                  receipt is gone; one tiny drop falls on its roof into a thimble-sized bucket
                  beside it. A tiny plink. TEXT: "as-is. bucket and all."
           JMSN: Truth? Cash offers usually come in lower than listing. Ours too.
           SHOW → JMSN single, steady, no cutaway.
           AVA: Wait. Really?
           SHOW → AVA single, eyebrows up.
           JMSN: That's why lower isn't everything. No commission. No fees. No closing costs. You
                 pick the closing date.
           SHOW → JMSN counts the three "no"s on his fingers; one TEXT pop per term. On "You pick
                  the closing date", AVA turns, uncaps a marker and circles one day on a wall

[CLOSE B]  JMSN: On you? It was never on you. It was "fix it first."
           SHOW → JMSN single, warm, steady eye contact. No cutaway. No plink under this line:
                  the room is completely quiet. Then AVA's reaction: a slow exhale, hand to her
                  chest. TEXT: "never on you"

[payload]  JMSN: Take what matters. Leave the rest.
           SHOW → AVA stands, lifts one framed photo off the bookshelf, tucks it under her arm,
                  and leaves everything else exactly as it is.

[CLOSE A]  AVA: Cash for my house... and the bucket stays?
           JMSN: The bucket stays. That's the Walk-Away Offer.
           SHOW → The wide shot again, same framing as 0.8 s. AVA sits back down, photo under
                  her arm. PLINK. Both look at the bucket and laugh.

[payload]  JMSN: No obligation.
           SHOW → JMSN single, open palms, a small shrug.

[CTA]      AVA: Tap below and answer a few questions.
           SHOW → AVA turns from JMSN straight into the lens (the second and last line to
                  camera) and points down. TEXT: "tap below" + the LEARN MORE button.
```

## Sound plan
- No music bed. The room's own tone.
- **The plink is the rhythm:**
  - on the first frame;
  - right after "The roof." (the joke);
  - a tiny one in the miniature;
  - **none at all under CLOSE B** (the quiet is the emphasis);
  - one at CLOSE A, then the laugh.
- Voices: the fixed audio blocks from v1 (AVA: warm, slightly husky, playful, easy pace; JMSN: calm, low, unhurried; podcast mics close, a small furnished room, very little echo). About 180 words per minute or slower; no speed-up.
