## Angle card (playbook §2)
```text
Avatar:                  Fran, the house that needs everything (research-cards.md, card 4)
False belief:            "The house got away from me, and I can't afford to catch it up." [D]
Villain:                 the "fix it first" rule of a regular sale [R: card 4, realtors say fix first]
Absolution line:         "Your fault? It was never you. It was 'fix it first.'"
Mechanism of the problem: a regular sale needs a buyer to fall in love with the house,
                         so you fix it first, with your money, before anyone pays you.
Mechanism of our fix:    we don't need anyone to fall in love; we buy it as it is.
The fix (registry §1):   as it is; take what matters, leave the rest; no commission, no fees,
                         no closing costs; you pick the closing date; no obligation;
                         the approved catch ("Cash offers usually come in lower than listing. Ours too.")
Emotion line:            embarrassment → relief → dignity
Format:                  two-presenter company podcast. AVA asks what sellers won't say out loud
                         (the Lem "confessor", but as hypotheticals, never as a seller);
                         JMSN, our buyer, answers (the Lem "guide").
Signature image:         the repair quote as a physical villain: read twice, folded into a drawer,
                         then unrolling across the floor in black and white; colour comes back
                         at the absolution.
Reward word for the hook: Cash
```

## The script
```text
[OPEN A]   AVA: Cash for your house, as it is? Leaky roof and all?
           SHOW → AVA close single at our podcast table (sunny living room of a lived-in house,
                  two mics on arms, mugs, a lamp). She leans on her forearms toward the mic, head
                  tilted, eyebrows up, a disbelieving half-smile. At "Leaky roof and all?" cut to
                  JMSN's single: a slow nod. TEXT (first frame): "cash, as it is?"

[payload]  JMSN: Even with the stain on the ceiling. Even with the bucket under the drip.
           SHOW → B-roll, JMSN's voice under: a brown water stain on a white ceiling, then a
                  plastic bucket catching a drip on a bare floor. Objects only.

[OPEN B]   AVA: Okay, but be honest. If that's my house... isn't that MY fault?
           SHOW → AVA single, softer: looks down at her mug, then back up at him. TEXT: "my fault?"

[payload]  JMSN: Then somebody tells you: fix it first. You get the quote. You read it twice.
                 Back in the drawer.
           SHOW → Black and white starts here. A handwritten repair quote on a kitchen counter
                  (handwriting too small to read, no numbers visible); it is turned over, then
                  folded into a kitchen drawer, and the drawer slides shut. No hands in frame.
           AVA: Easy for them to say.
           SHOW → AVA single, a small eye-roll and a half-laugh (a reaction clip).

[OPEN C]   JMSN: There's one reason "fix it first" never fit a house like yours.
           SHOW → JMSN single, calm, leaning back, one finger up. TEXT: "one reason"

[payload]  AVA: Let me guess. The roof?
           JMSN: Not the roof.
           SHOW → AVA single, a playful guess with a tilt of the head; cut to JMSN single
                  shaking his head with a small smile.

[CLOSE C]  JMSN: That one reason? A regular sale needs a buyer to FALL IN LOVE with it. So you
                 fix it first, with your money, before anyone pays you.
           SHOW → JMSN single on "That one reason?". Then the villain as an object, in black
                  and white: the repair quote unrolls off the counter and across the hallway
                  floor like a long receipt (writing unreadable). TEXT: "fix it first" →
                  "with your money"

[payload]  AVA: So what's different?
           JMSN: We don't need anyone to fall in love. We buy it as it is. The roof, the drip,
                 the stain. Take what matters. Leave the rest.
           SHOW → AVA single, curious. Then colour comes back: the same ceiling-stain shot,
                  same framing, now in warm colour. Then a wall with one lighter rectangle and
                  an empty nail where a framed photo was taken down; the rest of the room stays
                  as it is. TEXT: "as it is"

[CLOSE B]  JMSN: Your fault? It was never you. It was "fix it first."
           SHOW → JMSN single, warm, steady eye contact. No cutaway (trust line); the room goes
                  quiet. Then AVA's reaction: a slow exhale, hand to her chest. TEXT: "never you"

[payload]  JMSN: Truth? Cash offers usually come in lower than listing. Ours too.
           SHOW → JMSN single, steady, no cutaway.
           AVA: Wait. Really?
           SHOW → AVA single, surprised.
           JMSN: That's why lower isn't everything. No commission. No fees. No closing costs.
                 You pick the closing date. That's the deal.
           SHOW → One object per term, TEXT pop on each: a closed folder on a kitchen table
                  ("no commission"), an empty kitchen counter ("no fees"), a pen lying beside a
                  closed folder ("no closing costs"), a wall calendar with one day circled, no
                  numbers readable ("you pick the date"). Back to JMSN on "That's the deal."

[CLOSE A]  AVA: So... cash for my house, leaky roof and all?
           JMSN: As it is. That's how.
           SHOW → The one wide two-shot: both at the table. AVA leans in; JMSN slides a plain
                  closed folder across the table to her (the handoff); she opens it and smiles.

[payload]  JMSN: No obligation.
           SHOW → JMSN single, open palms, a small shrug.

[CTA]      AVA: Tap below and answer a few questions.
           SHOW → AVA turns from JMSN straight into the lens (the only line to camera) and
                  points down. TEXT: "tap below" + the LEARN MORE button.
```

## Production notes (for the prompts, not generated)
- **Voices** (playbook §9b): one fixed audio block per presenter, pasted unchanged into every clip.
  - **AVA:** "She speaks in a warm, slightly husky American voice, early thirties, relaxed and a little playful, at an easy conversational pace with natural pauses. The voice sounds like it comes from a podcast mic close to her mouth in a small living room with soft furnishings and very little echo. When she leans back from the mic, her voice gets a little quieter."
  - **JMSN:** "He speaks in a calm, low American voice, mid thirties, unhurried and sure, like he has seen every kind of house. The voice sounds like it comes from a podcast mic close to his mouth in the same small living room, very little echo. When he leans back, his voice gets a little quieter."
- **Pace budget:** about 180 words per minute or slower; no speed-up in the edit.
- **Sound:** no music bed; the room's own tone. At CLOSE B everything drops to room tone for a beat.
- **Clips:** one clip per line or reaction, each 4-5 s (J's rule). Hooks and closes on the faces. Payload lines that name a thing get the object b-roll; reactions stay on the face. AVA's clips use `12_AI_Characters/Ava/Ad20_Ava_look_D_v1.png` (+ face crop) as @Image1; JMSN uses `13_Generated/images/Ad16_JMSN_character_sheet_B_CHOSEN.png`.
- **B-roll:** objects and places only (rule of 2026-09-26). Candidates to frame-check before use: `07_Assets/Stock_Video/ceiling_4882745.mp4`, `leak_*` (`leak_6813965` shows people, so it's out). **3 fixes for the next script**
1. "A regular sale needs a buyer to FALL IN LOVE with it." → once the owner adds a registry line, swap in the sourced cause (buyers who borrow need the lender's check on the house). That turns the mechanism from a metaphor into a fact.
