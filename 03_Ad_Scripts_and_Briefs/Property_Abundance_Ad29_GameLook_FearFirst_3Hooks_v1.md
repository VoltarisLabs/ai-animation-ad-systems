# Property Abundance Ad29 | Game-look, fear-first | three hook variants v1

*Written 2026-10-14. Three new game-look ("GTA style") scripts, built as hook variants of the campaign winner: the Irene toll-road cut (`Irene_TollRoad_v5.mp4`, "vid 4 GTA" in the Full Cash Offer 10/7 campaign, 4.99% CTR and about a fifth of the UGC ad's cost per link click over the first week). Each keeps the winner's look and devices (photoreal game render, MISSION banner, THE SALE bar, toasts, NEW AREA UNLOCKED, YOUR HOUSE map end card) and changes the first frame, the first line and the character. Rules applied: `11_Psychological_Hooks/sell-fear.md` (on branch `sell-fear-landscape`, not yet on main), `sell-the-destination.md`, `claims-registry.md` re-read today, `01_Skills_and_SOPs/Flow_Animated_Story_Ads/slash_commands/gta-style/SKILL.md`. Untested.*

**Facts used (fact bank only; a source card on screen for each):** 42-year median home age (NAHB, 2026); insurance +24% 2021-2024 (Consumer Federation of America); first-time buyers 21%, lowest on record (NAR, 2025).

**Registry check:** neutral verbs only ("the house sells as-is"); no offer or closing timeline; no "any condition"; no business numbers; no "we buy" and no verb that makes the company the buyer; the Ask is "Ask for a cash offer. No obligation."; every call-out asks about the house, the policy or the map, never the person. Walt, June and Marie are third-person story characters, not testimonials. No company name. Section 6 blocker still applies: on 2026-10-14 `propertyabundanceusa.com` returned HTTP 403 on http and a certificate error on https.

**HUD note:** the landscape fact cards carry a number with a source line (sell-fear rule 1, owner rule 2026-10-09). That overrides the game-look skill's older "no digits" note for these cards only. HUD counters, prices and value meters stay out. The meter is Irene's THE SALE bar (no new label to clear): it drains while she waits and refills on her path.

**Length:** the fear-first shape runs about 40-45 s (the owner's worked example is 134 words / 47 s). These are 112-121 spoken words. A 33-s trim breaks the PAIN or DESTINATION budget, so run them at length.

## Why three, and what each tests

Playbook §5 round 2: three openings on the winning angle, same body shape, decided by hook rate. A = the house is the clock (home age). B = the pile (carrying cost). C = the empty map (no buyers). Each hook changes the first frame and the first line, not just the words (Meta creative-differentiation guidance). Names: `261014_Fran_HouseAge_GameLook_H1`, `261014_Fran_Pile_GameLook_H2`, `261014_Maya_EmptyMap_GameLook_H3`.

## Production notes

- Character sheet and house first (Nano Banana Pro), attached to every clip; add "must not resemble any real person". Same render style words as the Irene cut (photoreal game look), not the painted Ray look.
- First second: motion toward the lens or something happening (the aging street, the envelope through the slot, the map pan). Test two hook motions on the cheap model before paying for the final.
- Set the Meta thumbnail from the eye-contact frame (about 1 s in), not frame 0.
- Fact cards: plain data cards with the source line. No mastheads, no outlet logos, no recreated headlines.
- No HUD buttons or menus. (The Rosa cut carries a REPLAY button; Meta's non-existent-functionality rule.)
- Voice: one narrator file, consent filed in `PERMISSIONS.md`; synthesized effects only, or licensed ones listed there.
- Edit: `scripts/build_hud_ad.py` with Irene's bar and banners; captions below the face; minimap above the captions.
- Blocks before launch: owner review of the three characters; the landing page (registry Section 6); the conversion event on whatever page the campaign sends clicks to.
