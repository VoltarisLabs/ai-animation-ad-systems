HOW VEO SPEECH WORKS HERE
- Write: The man says: line goes here     (no quote marks; quote marks make Veo burn subtitles into the picture)
- One action and at most one spoken line per generation. Veo ignores multi-shot lists.
- Never write "iPhone" or any phone name. Veo draws a fake camera app on screen.
- Generate each clip, then cut it short in CapCut. That is where the fast cutting comes from.
