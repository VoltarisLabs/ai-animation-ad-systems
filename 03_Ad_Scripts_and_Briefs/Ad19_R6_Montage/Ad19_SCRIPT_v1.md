## Loop sequence

OPEN A > payload > OPEN B > payload > OPEN C > payload > CLOSE C > payload > CLOSE B > payload > CLOSE A > payload > CTA

- It matches the required order in `loop-order-rule`. There is a payload after every open and every close. No loop opens and closes on consecutive beats.
- **A** "Nobody takes a cut." is closed last by the list of costs plus the bonus, the biggest payoff.
- **B** "you might think your house is too rough" is closed by "I'm still buying it, just the way it is."
- **C** "here's your to-do list" is closed by "Just the address."
- The CTA calls back to the hook ("I'll send you a number" becomes "I'll send you that number").
