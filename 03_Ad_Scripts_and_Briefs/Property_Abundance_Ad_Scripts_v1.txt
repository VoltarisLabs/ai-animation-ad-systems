PROPERTY ABUNDANCE – UGC AD SCRIPTS v1
Format: 9:16 vertical, about 33 seconds, voiceover or real founder on camera, big bold captions (1 to 4 words per card, one keyword in color).
New shot about every 2 seconds. Every shot shows what the line says. Last shot = the real form on a phone + "Property Abundance".
No cash piles. No money counters. No actors or AI people claiming to be past customers. No numbers on screen.

============================================================
AD 1 – "THE PROOF PROBLEM"
Concept: The honest cash buyer (Winning Concept #1)
Persona: The skeptic (anyone who has seen "we buy houses" ads)
Hook 1: Contrarian | Hook 2: Pattern Interrupt
Term redefined: "fair offer" = a number you can check, not the biggest number
============================================================

[0-3s]
ON SCREEN: A LOWBALL ISN'T THE PROBLEM
VISUAL: A hand crumples a "WE BUY HOUSES" flyer and drops it.
VO: A lowball offer isn't the problem. A hidden one is.

[3-9s]
ON SCREEN: WHAT DID THEY TAKE OUT?
VISUAL: A house, then its fixed-up version fades in. Cut to an offer sheet with one number and nothing else.
VO: Every cash buyer starts with what your house is worth fixed up. Few show you what they take out.

[9-14s]
ON SCREEN: NO PAPER. NO REASONS.
VISUAL: A phone call. Then a closing table: the number is crossed out and a lower one written in.
VO: A number on the phone. No paper. No reasons. Then it drops at closing.

[14-17s]
ON SCREEN: IT'S A PROOF PROBLEM
VISUAL: Close-up on the crossed-out number.
VO: That's not a price problem. That's a proof problem.

[17-25s]
ON SCREEN: WORTH FIXED UP / MINUS REPAIRS / MINUS OUR COSTS
VISUAL: A hand writes the three lines on a clean offer sheet, one per line of VO. No dollar amounts.
VO: A fair offer isn't the biggest number. It's one you can check. Worth fixed up, minus repairs, minus our costs and profit.

[25-30s]
ON SCREEN: ASK FOR PROOF
VISUAL: The seller at the kitchen table, reading the sheet, nodding.
VO: Stop asking cash buyers for their best price. Ask for proof. Even from us.

[30-33s]
ON SCREEN: SEE THE MATH ON YOUR HOUSE
VISUAL: Phone showing the Property Abundance form. Logo.
VO: Tap below. Property Abundance shows you the math.

============================================================
AD 2 – "THE DRIVE BACK"
Concept: Still paying for a house you don't want (Winning Concept #2)
Persona: Inherited Irene (inherited a parent's house, lives far away)
Hook 1: Labeling | Hook 2: Reframe
Term redefined: "keeping the house" = paying every month to put off one choice
============================================================

[0-4s]
ON SCREEN: MOM'S HOUSE SITS EMPTY
VISUAL: An empty house at dusk. One porch light. Mail piled at the door.
VO: If Mom's house sits empty and you still pay for it, this is you.

[4-8s]
ON SCREEN: TAXES. INSURANCE. THE DRIVE.
VISUAL: A tax bill, an insurance letter, then a car on a long highway, one shot each.
VO: Taxes. Insurance. The long drive back when something breaks.

[8-13s]
ON SCREEN: ANOTHER SATURDAY GONE
VISUAL: A woman walks into a quiet kitchen with old family photos on the fridge.
VO: Another Saturday gone. You stand in her kitchen. You still can't sell.

[13-16s]
ON SCREEN: IT'S A GOODBYE PROBLEM
VISUAL: Close-up of her hand on the counter.
VO: That's not a house problem. That's a goodbye problem.

[16-21s]
ON SCREEN: KEEPING IT ISN'T KEEPING HER
VISUAL: She takes one photo off the fridge and puts it in her bag.
VO: Keeping the house isn't keeping her. It's paying every month to put off one choice.

[21-26s]
ON SCREEN: TAKE WHAT MATTERS. LEAVE THE REST.
VISUAL: She walks out with one small box. The house stays as it is behind her.
VO: You don't have to fix it or clean it out. Take what matters. Leave the rest.

[26-29s]
ON SCREEN: SELLING ISN'T THE GOODBYE
VISUAL: Her car pulling away in daylight, heading home.
VO: Selling isn't the goodbye. The drive back is.

[29-33s]
ON SCREEN: IT'S FREE TO ASK
VISUAL: Phone showing the Property Abundance form. Logo.
VO: Property Abundance buys houses as they are. Tap below. It's free to ask.
