PART 1 – HOW TO DRIVE GEMINI WEB SO IT STOPS MAKING MISTAKES
============================================================

BEFORE YOU PASTE ANYTHING
1. Set aspect ratio in the UI, not in the prompt. Every clip you generated so far came
   back 1280x720 landscape even though the prompt said 9:16. Veo 3.1 supports native 9:16,
   but the app's own setting wins. Find the ratio control and set 9:16 before prompting.
2. Set duration to 8 s in the UI. Veo 3.1 offers 4, 6 or 8. Do not ask for 10 or 16 in text.
3. Set resolution to 1080p if the control exists.

ONE CLIP PER CHAT
4. Start a NEW chat for every clip. In a long chat Gemini carries over the previous clip's
   framing and wardrobe and blends them. Fresh chat, one prompt, one video.
5. Never send two clips in one message. It makes one video and ignores the second.

PASTE IT RAW
6. Paste the clip block exactly as written. Do not add "please" or "can you". Do not let it
   rewrite your prompt - if it replies with a revised version and asks to confirm, say:
   "Use my text exactly as written." Then resend.
7. If it asks a clarifying question, do not answer in conversation. Resend the same block.
   Answering in chat adds your answer to the prompt and drifts the shot.

THE FOUR THINGS THAT BROKE LAST TIME, AND THE FIX
8. It wrote text on screen three times (garbled clipboard, "1.5 m" labels, gibberish page).
   Cause: any noun near paper, and any number anywhere, gets drawn into the picture.
   Fix: these prompts contain no numbers except shot timings, and no describable surface.
9. It changed his shirt mid-ad (navy henley to white polo) and changed streets.
   Fix: the character block and location block are repeated word for word in every clip.
   Do not shorten them to save typing. That repetition is the lock.
10. It added dialogue you did not ask for. Veo 3.1 generates audio natively.
    Fix: every clip ends with "Nobody speaks. No dialogue. No voices." Keep that line.
11. It made one long slow push-in instead of your cuts.
    Fix: max two shots per clip, each one continuous action. Never list four shots.

AFTER EACH GENERATION
12. Run the reject checklist in PART 4 before you keep it. Rejecting costs one generation.
    Shipping a bad clip costs the whole ad.
13. Save as gemini_clipNN.mp4 and send it over. Measured, not eyeballed.

PART 4 – REJECT CHECKLIST (run on every clip before keeping it)
============================================================
Reject and regenerate if any of these are true:
  1. Any readable word, number or logo anywhere in frame.
  2. A camera app interface, shutter button, PHOTO/VIDEO label or zoom badge.
  3. Glossy look: soft glow, blurred background, graded colour.
  4. Plastic skin: no pores, no lines, even tone, airbrushed.
  5. Anything morphing, sliding on its own, appearing or disappearing.
  6. Wrong number of fingers, or impossible finger movement.
  7. Anyone's mouth moving as if speaking, or any voice on the audio.
  8. A static object held more than about 3 s with nothing moving.
  9. A dissolve, fade or morph instead of a hard cut.
  10. The sheet in CLIP 8 showing any mark at all.
  11. Landscape instead of vertical. Fix the UI setting, not the prompt.
