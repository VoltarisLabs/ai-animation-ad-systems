PART 4 – REJECT CHECKLIST (run on every clip before you keep it)
============================================================
Reject and regenerate if any of these are true:
  1. Any readable word, number or logo anywhere in frame.
  2. A camera app interface, shutter button, PHOTO/VIDEO label or zoom badge.
  3. Glossy, smooth, "cinematic" look: soft glow, shallow background blur, graded colour.
  4. Plastic skin: no pores, no lines, even tone, airbrushed.
  5. Anything morphing, sliding on its own, appearing or disappearing.
  6. A hand with the wrong number of fingers, or impossible finger movement.
  7. Anyone's mouth moving as if speaking.
  8. A static object held for more than about 3 s with nothing moving.
  9. A dissolve, fade or morph transition instead of a hard cut.
  10. The sheet in CLIP 8 showing any mark at all.
