## Built on the Skool Hall of Fame formula

Read from the transcripts of all 6 Hall of Fame singing ads plus the course example (2026-10-12). The client's best performer is "Crochet Song for the win": 61.486 s, 120 words, "performed the best out of all 4". Its formula:

1. The hook is a raw, embarrassing, specific confession.
2. Daily pain in short lines.
3. Failed fixes, with a number.
4. The old fix is the villain, shown with a vivid metaphor.
5. One simple routine.
6. A specific funny payoff that calls back the pain.
7. A short tag.

The first-person "I" here is the house, a talking object. Claims registry Section 4 bans an AI character telling her own seller story.

A critic agent scored v2 of this draft 6/10 against the best ad; all 6 of its rewrites are in this version.

### Edit rules

- **Captions:** word by word, bold white capitals with a dark outline, one size and one position, about 60% down. Taken from this lyric text, timed with Whisper.

## Fast edit plan (user's ask, 2026-10-12: not like AI, cards, face zoom-ins, nothing held over 1-2 s)

**Shot plan:** `10_Video_Builds/Ad27_Song_Ruthie/shot_plan_v5.md`, generated by `shot_plan_v5.py`.
- 102 shots over 158 beats = 98.750 s on the 96 BPM grid (1 beat = 0.625 s).
- Longest single shot is 2 beats = 1.250 s; the average shot is 0.968 s.
- Shot types: 29 macro, 25 wide, 19 face zoom-ins, 12 medium, 9 house lip-sync, 8 card.
- When the real Suno take exists, snap every cut to its beats and lyric times. The beat counts stay, the seconds follow the song.

**Cut rules**
- Default shot 2 beats (1.25 s). Montage shots 1 beat (0.625 s). Never longer than 2 beats; if a moment needs more, split it with a punch-in, a cut-away or a card.
- **Face zoom-ins** come in two kinds, on every reaction and every house line:
  - Snap zoom: 100% to 140% in 3 frames, on the beat.
  - Crash zoom: a fast push over 1 beat.
- **Beat punch-ins** (Vinz): +10% scale on each beat in the montages (roofers, the walking leak, BAM, the sandcastle).
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat, a white flash on lightning and the sun, a whip pan on the walking leak. No dissolves or morph transitions.

**Cards (all made in the edit, never drawn by the AI)**
- **Source cards:** a cream fabric tag with a stitched border and black type. It slides up in 6 frames and stays over 2 shots: "42 YEARS · Median age of owner-occupied homes · NAHB, 2026", later the "43" callback in the same style.
- **Offer stamps:** red ink stamps that hit with a thud and a 105% bounce: SELLS AS-IS, TAKE WHAT MATTERS, YOU PICK THE DATE, NO CLEAN-OUT.
- **Roofer tally:** stitched tally marks (no digits) that add one mark per roofer.
- **BAM!:** a yellow starburst with a punch-in on "bam".
- **Lyric captions:** word by word, bold white capitals, dark outline, one size, fixed at about 60% height.
- **End card:** builds on the beat, one line per beat (CASH OFFER, then each term, then "No obligation." in yellow), so the frame keeps changing.

**Not like AI (checked shot by shot)**
1. **One locked crochet style and cast sheets on every generation.** Reject any clip where a face, the house or a hand drifts, melts or changes.
2. **Short use of every take.** We never use more than 1.25 s of a clip in one piece. Each 5 s clip gives 2-3 shots (wide, then face zoom, then macro). About 40-50 clips for 102 shots (estimate).
3. **Handmade motion.** Character animation is stepped to 12 fps in the edit so it reads as stop-motion crochet. Punch-ins and pans stay smooth.
4. **Real camera language.** Macro lens with shallow focus, rack focus, slight handheld shake added in the edit. No slow floating drifts and no "cinematic" glow.
5. **Real texture and light.** Visible stitches, yarn fuzz, slight asymmetry, warm practical lamps inside and cool rain light outside. Colour graded slightly down, never oversaturated.
6. **Real sound on every gag:** drip plinks on the beat, hail clatter, shingle whoosh, boing, bonk, splash, squirt, phone ring, BAM, jingle, thunder. Mixed under the vocal.
7. **No AI-drawn text anywhere.** Calendars, estimates and signs are prompted blank or angled away.
