### Edit rules (reference fixes + Vinz)

- **Captions:** word-by-word karaoke in a white rounded pill with dark text; the sung word goes bold.
  - One size and one position, centred at about 60% down the frame (out of the bottom safe zone).
  - Captions come from this lyric text, timed by Whisper word times.
- **Cuts:** on the beat. Never trim the music in a way that breaks the tempo.
- **Instrumental gaps:** always have something moving (the bucket drums in S9).
- **Effects:** +10% punch-in on beat hits in S3, S8 and S9. Saturation drops slightly in the PRESENT scenes. No glitches; they'd fight the ballad.
- **Shot types:** the house lip-syncs in S1, S5, S6, S10, S14 and S17. Every other shot is a story shot with a no-dialogue prompt.
