## Built on the Skool Hall of Fame formula

Read from the transcripts of all 6 Hall of Fame singing ads plus the course example (2026-10-12). The client's best performer is "Crochet Song for the win": 61.486 s, 120 words, "performed the best out of all 4". Its formula:

1. The hook is a raw, embarrassing, specific confession.
2. Daily pain in short lines.
3. Failed fixes, with a number.
4. The old fix is the villain, shown with a vivid metaphor.
5. One simple routine.
6. A specific funny payoff that calls back the pain.
7. A short tag.

The first-person "I" here is the house, a talking object. Claims registry Section 4 bans an AI character telling her own seller story.

A critic agent scored v2 of this draft 6/10 against the best ad; all 6 of its rewrites are in this version.

### Edit rules
