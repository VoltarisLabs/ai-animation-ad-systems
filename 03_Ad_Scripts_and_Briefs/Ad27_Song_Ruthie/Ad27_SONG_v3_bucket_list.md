# Ad 27 v3: "Bucket List" (song ad, full script)

Status: full script written 2026-10-12, after the user rejected v2 ("don't copy this directly"). Nothing generated yet.

## The idea

Linda's "bucket list" isn't Paris. It's the four buckets catching the leaks in her house. Once the house sells as it is, the same chorus turns into her real bucket list.

- **Format:** an original concept, no lines or devices lifted from any reference. 0 shared four-word phrases with all 13 Skool reference ads (checked by script).
- **What it borrows from the Skool references (principles only):** one picture repeated as the chorus; a turn line; the second chorus mirrored into her new life; visual gags on the beat; a singer on camera for the hooks.
- **Singer:** a felt narrator singer (third person) plus the buckets and the grandkids singing the echo. No AI character tells her own "I sold my house" story (claims registry Section 4).

## Visuals

### Style lock (every image and video prompt)

- Needle-felted wool miniature world with visible fibres and soft wool texture. This is the material of the community's most-liked singing ad; the characters and story are our own.
- Warm, handmade light. Vertical 9:16.
- No text, letters or numbers in frame; all text goes on in the edit.

### Cast (make these sheets first; attach them to every scene)

- **Linda:** felt woman about 64 (typical seller age, NAR 2025):
  - Silver curly bob, round tortoiseshell glasses.
  - Mustard cardigan over a denim shirt, fuzzy pink slippers.
  - Big expressive felt eyebrows.
- **The narrator singer:** felt country singer with big auburn hair, a rhinestone denim jacket and an acoustic guitar. She pops up inside Linda's scenes and sings to camera.
- **The four buckets:** red, blue, yellow and green felt buckets. Each has a small felt mouth that only appears when it sings the "(bucket list!)" echo.
- **Supporting cast:**
  - The contractor: felt man with a clipboard and a cap.
  - Her adult daughter.
  - Two grandkids: a girl about 4 and a boy about 7.
- **The house:** a tired 1980s two-story felt house.

### Edit rules

- **Captions:** word by word, bold white capitals with a dark outline. One size and one position, centred about 60% down (out of the bottom safe zone). Taken from this lyric text and timed with Whisper word times.
- **Cuts:** a new picture about every 1.5 s, always on the beat. In the chorus, each bucket snap is one beat, with a +10% punch-in.
- **Tempo:** never trim the music in a way that breaks it. During any instrumental gap something moves (the buckets drip on the beat).
- **Lip-sync shots:** S5, S8, S13 and S19 (the narrator), plus the "(bucket list!)" echoes (the buckets, then the grandkids). Every other shot is a story shot with a no-dialogue prompt.
