**Audience:** Persona 3, the Burned-Out Landlord. The tenant is finally out and left the rental trashed, and the mortgage keeps coming.
**Format:** AI UGC handheld selfie, 9:16. Three spots inside one vacant rental. 3 Omni clips of 8 / 8 / 10 s.

**Discrepancy to know about ("we buy it ourselves").** The project CLAUDE.md lists "Property Abundance buys the house itself (no wholesaling)" as confirmed by the user on 2026-09-25, and the Ad 16 final used "WE'RE the buyer". But the Ad 16 v3 breakdown banned "we buy it ourselves" as an open question, the research doc still marks the offer terms PLACEHOLDER, and the Hook Map's truth gate lists "direct purchase" as unconfirmed. **This ad follows CLAUDE.md.** Please reconfirm it once, and then update the research doc so the files agree.

**Why Solution-aware.**
1. He knows his problem. He is standing in it.
2. He already knows the ways out: re-rent, fix and list, list as-is, or sell to a cash buyer. The research lists every one of them as something landlords try.
3. The Hook Map places him at solution-aware and says he "has already considered cash buyers" and has probably been warned about wholesalers.
4. He is not most-aware. He doesn't know Property Abundance, and a search for the name finds the UK training company first (research doc, "Name conflict").

So the ad opens on the result he wants (to be out), not on education. It sells against his alternatives (Stage 4) and shows the mechanism with confirmed terms only: as-is, we buy it ourselves, no commission, no fees. Checklist Step 1 table for this level: open with the result or the mechanism, using the BAB/AIDA shape.

## Part B. Script breakdown (H1, the main hook)

| # | Beat | Spoken line | Job of this line | Source (research quote or rule) | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Open A** (hook) | "The tenant left. Now YOU can too." | Qualify him in the first second (the word "tenant") and promise the one thing he buys: an exit. It opens a loop: how? | Hook Map, Jason lever 3: "Finality. The promise is not money, it's 'it stops being yours.'" Research desire: "Get out." Sabri: benefit-led. | Curiosity + benefit, permission to stop |
| 2 | Payload | "House sits empty. Same mortgage, every month." | Turn the bleed into a picture, in monthly terms with no number | Hook Map lever 2: "Arithmetic of the bleed. He thinks in monthly loss. Speak in it." Research outcome: "Stop the money leak." | Loss aversion (accurate) |
| 3 | **Open B** | "So what's the move?" | Say out loud the decision he's stuck on | Hook Map: "He does not need convincing that selling is right." Jeremy Haynes: he's comparing options | Curiosity gap |
| 4 | Payload | "Patch THAT and bet on the NEXT tenant?" | Alternative 1 and its real cost: another tenant | Research: "managing tenants... could just turn into another thing on my plate." README voice: "I don't want to deal with another tenant." | Contrast |
| 5 | Payload | "Fix it, list it, pay commission?" | Alternative 2 and its real cost: repairs, then a commission | Research: "Fixing it up first: No money, time or energy to manage contractors"; misconception table (commission) | Contrast |
| 6 | **Close B** | "Or sell it AS-IS." | Answer "what's the move" with the third road | Confirmed: buys as-is. `offer-and-mechanism.md`: "A buyer who buys it as it stands" | Mechanism |
| 7 | Payload | "We buy it ourselves, for LESS than a fixed-up sale." | Two trust moves in one breath: we're not a wholesaler, and the catch is said first | Research objection: "just wholesalers who tie you up in contract." Hook Map: "Self-incrimination... the one move that outperforms on all three." Confirmed + approved claims | Damaging admission |
| 8 | Payload | "But you pay no commission, no fees." | Take back the cost he named in line 5 | Confirmed: no commission, no fees or closing costs | Risk reversal, contrast |
| 9 | **Close A** | "They left. Now YOU can." | Pay off the hook, now that the how is explained | Loop rule: A closes last with the biggest payoff | Consistency |
| 10 | CTA | "Tap below." | Smallest honest step. The end card adds LEARN MORE · NO OBLIGATION. | `copy-banks.md`: a soft CTA for cold traffic. Confirmed: no obligation. | Zero-price first step |

**Loop sequence:** OPEN A (1) → payload (2) → OPEN B (3) → payload (4, 5) → CLOSE B (6) → payload (7, 8) → CLOSE A (9) → CTA (10). They are nested and close in reverse order. No loop opens and closes back to back. When B closes, A is still open. The hook's promise closes last.

**Why only 2 loops:** the ad runs about 23 s. The psychology report says under 30 s gets 1 loop and 30-60 s gets A + B, and Howie (00:25:08) says a 30 s ad has no time for A, B and C. Two loops follow the brief and Ad 16's precedent. At this length, the report would call even B a stretch.

### reel_direction_2 mapping (the skill preloaded for this job)
- **QUESTION (silent):** "Do I have to fix this place before I can get out of it?"
- **Hook 1 type:** Curiosity Gap (H1). H3 is the Reframe type if you want the skill's own archetype.
- **Where this ad departs from the skill, and why:**
  - Hook 2 (Close A) echoes Hook 1 instead of being a different type. The project template says Close A pays off the hook for consistency, and Ad 16 v3 did the same.
  - There is no ILLUSTRATE diagnostic line or redefined term. Neither fits in 60 words, and H3 carries the diagnostic line as a testable hook.
- **Language gate:** zero em dashes and zero banned phrases (grep run on this file). Tension lands in the first 5 words ("The tenant left. Now YOU").
