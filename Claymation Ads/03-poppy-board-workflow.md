**Source:** Ad Creators Lab lesson
**Saved:** 2026-08-29

> ⚠️ **Requires a Poppy subscription (monthly, paid).** Not currently held. See "Do we need Poppy?" below.

---

## What you need

### 1. Script
The full ad script you want to turn into claymation.

### 2. Product info
- Website link
- **Screenshots of the product**
- Key benefits / what it does

### 3. Poppy Board
- Get the board from the course link (**requires a Poppy subscription**)
- 🚨 **Copy the board to your own account** before using it

---

## The 7 steps

### Step 1 — Add your inputs
Put in:
- script
- product info
- **style references**

### Step 2 — Turn script into scenes
Ask AI to:
- break the script into scenes
- describe what each scene looks like

### Step 3 — Turn scenes into prompts
For each scene, ask AI to create an **image prompt**.

### Step 4 — Generate images
Paste prompts into your image tool. **One image per scene.**

### Step 5 — Fix consistency
- Make sure the same character looks the same
- **Reuse references if needed**

### Step 6 — Organise scenes
- Put images in order
- **Make sure the story flows**

### Step 7 — Animate (next step)
Turn images into video.

## What this lesson adds that the others don't

**1. Product screenshots as a required input.** The other lessons say "script + product info." This one explicitly wants **screenshots of the product**. That matters — the crochet and skeleton skills both say to upload the real product photo as a second reference so the product renders accurately. Same principle, stated up front here.

**2. Style references as a Step-1 input.** The Canva SOP never mentions style references; it relies on the loaded prompt document to carry the style. Poppy's board expects you to supply them. If we go the Poppy route we'd need to source claymation style refs (Pinterest, same as the Seedance UGC workflow).

**3. Step 6, organise and check story flow.** A review beat before animation. Worth keeping regardless of route — it's the cheapest place to catch a broken sequence, before any video credits burn.
