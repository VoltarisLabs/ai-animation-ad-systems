# Ad Scorecard (100 points)

Score every finished ad before it goes live. **Launch at 75 or more. Rewrite below 75. Any compliance fail blocks launch no matter the score.** Two people score it separately. If their scores differ by more than 10 points, discuss before launch.

| # | Category | Points | 0 = | Full points = |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | 20 | Generic, brand-first, slow start | Stops the right person in 1-3s. Scores 20+ on the 7-point hook check |
| 2 | **Audience and awareness fit** | 10 | Could be for anyone. Opening mismatched to awareness | One clear audience. Opening matches their awareness level |
| 3 | **One big idea** | 10 | Several promises, muddled | One promise, one angle, one offer |
| 4 | **Offer strength** | 15 | Vague "contact us" | Scores 16+/20 on the value equation. Low-risk first step. Real reason to act |
| 5 | **Proof and belief** | 15 | Claims with no support | Specific numbers, similar-customer proof, mechanism, guarantee |
| 6 | **Clarity** | 10 | Jargon, long sentences, needs rereading | Passes the 5-second test. Grade 5-7 reading level |
| 7 | **Specificity** | 5 | Adjectives ("best," "fast") | Numbers, names, times, places |
| 8 | **CTA** | 10 | Missing, multiple, or a big ask | One small, specific, first-person step right after the proof |
| 9 | **Voice and platform fit** | 5 | Feels like an ad, off-brand | Native to the platform, sounds like the brand, works with the sound off |
| | **Total** | **100** | | |

## Format checks (must pass)
**Short-form video**
- [ ] Three-layer hook (visual, spoken, text) in 0-2s.
- [ ] First frame works as a still.
- [ ] Makes sense with the sound off (burned-in captions).
- [ ] A visual change every 1-3s.
- [ ] Text inside the safe zone. End card with the CTA.

**Static**
- [ ] Passes the 1-second test.
- [ ] 8 words or fewer on the image, readable at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Text inside the safe zone for each size (4:5 and 9:16 built separately).
- [ ] Image text, primary text and headline tell one promise.

## Compliance gate (pass / fail)
- [ ] Every claim can be proven (keep the source in the brief).
- [ ] No fake scarcity, timers or testimonials.
- [ ] Follows the platform's rules for the category (health, finance, housing, employment, credit, social issues; see [platform-specs.md](platform-specs.md)).
- [ ] No personal attributes called out in a restricted way (e.g., "Are you overweight?" or "Do you have debt?" can get rejected on Meta).
- [ ] Required disclaimers are present.
- [ ] Landing page matches the ad's promise.

## Quick self-check (30 seconds)
1. Would the target customer stop scrolling?
2. Do they know what we offer in 5 seconds?
3. Do they believe it?
4. Do they know exactly what to do next?
5. Is anything in it untrue or exaggerated?
