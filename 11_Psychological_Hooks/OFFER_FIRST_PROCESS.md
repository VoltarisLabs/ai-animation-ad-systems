## The 11 steps

### 1. Pick the format first
| | A. Presenter walk-and-talk (like R1) | B. Voice-over montage (like R2) |
|---|---|---|
| On screen | JMSN or Emily in a selfie, walking, background always moving; cutaways on objects | A new clip every 1.2-2.3 s under the character's voice; no talking head |
| Cuts | R1: 1.40 per 10 s, 6 in 42.88 s | R2: 4.91 per 10 s; R3 4.12, R4 7.71 |
| Mean shot | 6.13 s | 1.24-2.32 s |
| Words per shot (median) | whole sentences on the face | 2-8 |
| Generation cost | Talking clips (Kie) | No talking clips: licensed footage + voice |

Format B cuts mid-sentence and uses people in b-roll. Both break today's standing rules, so use B only after the user changes those rules.

### 2. The hook names the offer or the pain, in plain words
- Say it in **3 s or less**. First word at 0.00 s.
- The viewer must hear **"buy", "sell", "offer" or "cash" by 4.5 s** (the references: 0.96-4.50 s).
- Pick one of four families (the proven reference is in brackets):

| Family | Shape | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | "Don't [costly thing] before you [our term]." | R1 "Don't sell your house without getting bonus cash." |
| Pain question + instant answer | "Why are you still [pain]? Here's what I'll do." | R2 "Why are you still paying the mortgage for a house you want to get rid of?" |
| Direct ask | "Send me the address. I'll [our offer]." | R3 "Send me your address and I'll show you what I can offer..." |
| Proof / "this house" | "I'd buy this house exactly like this." Needs the house on screen, and a real sale for any result claim | R4; our Ad 16 hook |

- **The 4.5-second kill check:** would a scrolling seller know by 4.5 s that this is someone who buys houses? If not, rewrite, however well the hook scores.
- Then run the normal kill checks and the 7-point score (`AD_COPY_CHECKLIST.md` Step 4).
- Loss framing is **not** proven better than gain framing (Ref_Ads_Research §2). Keep a gain-framed twin of every warning hook.

### 3. The first frame and the first cut
- **First frame, one of:**
  - the problem house in motion (a drive-by or walk-up POV);
  - a satisfying hands-at-work clip;
  - the payoff (keys, an offer in hand);
  - the character already walking.
- **First cut at 2.5-2.8 s** (the references: 2.50-2.77 s).
- R1 opens on 2 b-roll shots under the voice, and the face first appears at 7.08 s. Opening on b-roll is allowed.

### 4. The whole deal in one sentence by about 13 s
R2 says the deal and closes it with "That's the deal." at 12.66 s. Plain words: what we do, and on what terms.

### 5. The no-list: every confirmed term, one line each
- Say **every confirmed term that fits** the persona. The references say 6-10 negations each.
- Each term is its own short line, so each can get its own picture and caption.
- Check the current confirmed list in the project CLAUDE.md ("Open items"). On 2026-09-28 it was:
  - buys as-is (no repairs);
  - belongings can stay (no cleaning out);
  - no commission;
  - no fees or closing costs;
  - the seller picks the closing date;
  - no obligation;
  - Property Abundance buys the house itself.
- Count the terms used and write the count in the checks.

### 6. The CTA, twice
- **First CTA at 30-60% of the ad** (the references: 31.5-62.8% for R1-R3). **Second CTA at the end.**
- Name the exact steps: "Tap the button below. Answer a few questions."
- The second CTA can repeat the first in other words.

### 7. Urgency only if it is true
- No fake limits, caps, "slots" or timers (FTC dark patterns; checklist Step 6).
- Honest substitutes: "No obligation." "You pick the closing date."

### 8. Pictures follow the words
- Every line that names a thing gets that thing on screen, for about one phrase.
- Phone-shot look: handheld, natural light, real rooms. No studio look.
- Faces:
  - only the character, plus anything the current b-roll rule allows;
  - a reaction face goes on a payoff line only if the user has allowed people in b-roll;
  - never a person implied to be our seller.
- Frame-check every stock clip before it is assigned. Read any sign or paper in it for broker names and phone numbers.

### 9. Captions on every line
- 2-4 words per caption, one keyword in colour, carrying the spoken words.
- Keep every caption **between 14% and 65% from the top** of the frame: y = 269 to 1248 in 1080 × 1920. Meta's safe zone keeps the bottom 35% free.
- Before delivery, check a frame of every caption in the finished file.

### 10. Sound
- The voice leads, at -14 LUFS. R1-R3 use voice only.
- Music is optional. If you use it, follow the approved bed level in `ugc-edit-merge-then-scenes`.
- Reels play sound-on by default, so the voice has to carry the ad on its own.

### 11. Test
- Run the offer-first hook against the current best hook, on the same body.
- Read two numbers:
  - **hook rate** = 3-second video plays ÷ impressions (practitioners call 20-40% solid; there is no Meta or real-estate benchmark);
  - **hold rate** = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second plays.
- Log the winner in `swipe-file.md`.

## Can use / can't use

**Can use:**
- the four hook families;
- the no-list built from confirmed terms;
- a CTA that names its steps, said twice;
- the walking-selfie framing;
- 2-4 word captions.

**Never, unless the claim becomes confirmed and true:**
- speed ("24 hours", "7 days", "this week", "today");
- a "bonus", or paying the seller's mortgage or moving costs;
- caps, slots or "now or never";
- "sold in X days" or any result we can't show;
- the references' footage: other creators' people, other brokers' signs and phone numbers, bank branches.

## Script deliverable
1. The labelled code block (`[OPEN A]` ... `[CTA]`) with a `SHOW →` line under every line (`scripts-with-loop-labels`). Add the caption text on the SHOW line.
2. The loop sequence written out. It must pass `loop-order-rule`.
3. A clip plan: which lines go in which generation clip, with the word count and duration of each. The model's word limits apply: at 8 s, 18 words when the clip ends on a loop close, 19-21 otherwise.
4. A timing estimate at the measured pace, and the time at which the offer word, the deal, CTA 1 and CTA 2 land.
5. The pre-delivery checks below, each marked PASS or FAIL with its measured value.

## Pre-delivery checks
- [ ] The first word is spoken at 0.00 s, and the hook takes 3 s or less.
- [ ] "Buy", "sell", "offer" or "cash" is heard by 4.5 s.
- [ ] The whole deal is stated by about 13 s.
- [ ] The first cut lands at 2.5-2.8 s.
- [ ] Confirmed terms used: N of 7, each as its own line.
- [ ] CTA 1 lands at 30-60% of the ad, and CTA 2 at the end, both naming the steps.
- [ ] There is no speed, bonus, scarcity or result claim, and every claim is on the confirmed list.
- [ ] The loop order passes (nested or staggered, never back to back).
- [ ] Every line has a caption, placed between y = 269 and 1248.
- [ ] No person is shown or implied to be our seller or customer.
- [ ] The cuts follow the chosen format and the standing cut rule.
