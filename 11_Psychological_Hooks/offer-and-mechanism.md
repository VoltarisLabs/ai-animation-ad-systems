# Offer and Mechanism: What the Copy Sells

Copy can't rescue a weak offer. Sabri's most repeated line is that a compelling offer beats a convincing argument, and he names a weak offer as the first thing he fixes on any account. Georgi's version: a claim with no mechanism reads as hype. Build both **before** writing hooks.

Sources: [sabri-suby.md](03-modern-creators/sabri-suby.md) (Godfather Offer, hero mechanism, two-step funnel, radical transparency), [stefan-georgi.md](03-modern-creators/stefan-georgi.md) (unique mechanism, UMP/UMS, the brief). Every claim must be one you can prove.

---

## 1. The mechanism: why it works when other things didn't
Answer three questions (Sabri's hero mechanism, Georgi's UMP/UMS):
1. **Why do they have the problem?** This is the mechanism of the problem, the real cause they haven't been told.
2. **What have they already tried?**
3. **Why did that fall short, and how does ours work differently?** This is the mechanism of the solution.

Then:
- **Give it a short, plain name** so it can be repeated and remembered, and so it belongs to you.
- **Explain only enough cause and effect to be believed.** Georgi warns against step-by-step "science lecture" metaphors.
- **Don't reveal the whole mechanism in the ad.** If they can solve it without clicking, they won't click (Sabri's saffron-tea teardown). Tease the what; the page gives the how.

| Industry | Mechanism of the problem | Mechanism of the solution |
|---|---|---|
| Skincare | Serum sits on a dead-skin layer and never reaches the skin | Exfoliate first so the serum absorbs |
| B2B software | Reports are built weekly, so problems surface a week late | Live alerts the day a number moves |
| Home buying (as-is) | Retail buyers need the house fixed and emptied first | A buyer who buys it as it stands, belongings and all |

## 2. The offer stack (Godfather Offer, honest version)
Draft the most generous version you can imagine, then wind it back to what you can truly deliver.

| Part | What it does | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Rationale** | Says why the offer is this good | With no reason, people assume a catch. State how you make money. |
| **Value** | Itemises what they get, or no longer have to do | List the removed chores and costs too (repairs, fees, showings, setup time) |
| **Price / terms** | Set against the value | Compare with the real alternative, not a made-up "worth" figure |
| **Bonuses** | Raise response on their own | Two or three, each useful by itself |
| **Risk reversal** | Moves the risk to you | A guarantee, a free first step, or "no obligation" if true |
| **Scarcity** | Only if real | A real capacity limit or a real date. Never invent one. If you have none, leave it out. |

**Sabri's test:** if the offer doesn't make the founder a little nervous, it isn't strong enough. **Our test:** every line is true, and the team can deliver it every time.

**Proof and offer trade off** (Sabri): with little proof, the offer has to carry more of the weight. As proof builds, the offer can relax.

## 3. Radical transparency: say the catch first
Name the drawback before they can raise it. Early honesty about a negative makes everything else more believable. This matches the "Damaging admission" trigger in [triggers-index.md](triggers-index.md).
- "It's not the cheapest. It's the one you won't have to replace."
- "A cash offer is lower than a fixed-up retail sale. Here's what you don't have to pay or do to get it."
- "It takes 20 minutes to set up. After that you don't touch it."

## 4. Pick the funnel by awareness gap and price
| Situation | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Most aware, low risk | Ad → offer page | Nothing left to teach |
| Solution or product aware | Ad → page with mechanism and proof → action | They need the "why this one" |
| Problem aware, or a big decision | **Two-step:** ad → free value (guide, checklist, quiz, video) → offer, then nurture | Most people are "in the market but not ready." Let them raise a hand first (Sabri). |
| Unaware | Story or education content → retarget with the offer | You can't sell someone who hasn't named the problem |

**Congruence rule** (both creators): the next step keeps the ad's exact promise, format and emotional temperature. An article-style ad goes to an article-style page. A raw, emotional hook doesn't land on a cold corporate page.

## 5. Follow-up is part of the offer
- Sabri's own tracking found his most valuable buyers converted **90+ days** after first contact (his claim). Plan the follow-up before launch.
- Keep follow-ups around 80% value and 20% ask. Each touch should teach them something true about their situation.
- A "maybe" should get a clear next step, not silence. Never manufacture a deadline to force it.

## Done when
- [ ] The mechanism is written in two sentences and has a plain name.
- [ ] Every line of the offer stack is true and deliverable.
- [ ] The catch is written down and said first.
- [ ] The funnel shape matches the awareness gap.
- [ ] The first three follow-ups are drafted.
