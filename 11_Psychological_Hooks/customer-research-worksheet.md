# Customer Research Worksheet

Most ad copy should be found, not written (Wiebe). Budget about 60 minutes per new product or audience. It saves days of guessing.

## 1. Where to mine (collect 30+ quotes)
| Source | What to grab |
|---|---|
| Your reviews and testimonials | Results in their words, "I was worried that..." |
| Competitor reviews (3-star ones are gold) | What people wanted but didn't get |
| Amazon reviews of related books and products | Deep pains, desired identity |
| Reddit, Facebook groups, forums | Unfiltered frustrations, slang, what they've tried |
| Sales calls, DMs, support tickets | Objections word for word |
| Ad comments (yours and competitors') | Skepticism, questions, hot buttons |
| YouTube comments on niche videos | What they're hoping for |

Copy the quotes **exactly**, including slang and typos.

## 2. Sort the quotes
| Pains (what hurts) | Wants (the result) | Worries (why they won't buy) | Tried and failed | Exact phrases to steal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## 3. Profile of one real buyer
```
Name / role:            [e.g., "Dana, runs a 3-chair salon"]
Situation today:        [the day-to-day with the problem]
Trigger moment:         [what makes them search NOW]
Dream outcome:          [in their words]
Biggest fear:           [in their words]
Already tried:          [alternatives, and why they failed]
Who they trust:         [experts, peers, sources]
Identity they want:     [who they want to become]
Awareness level:        [Unaware / Problem / Solution / Product / Most aware]
Where they scroll:      [platforms, times]
```

## 4. Output
- Top 5 pains -> hook raw material.
- Top 3 worries -> objections the body must answer.
- Top 3 "tried and failed" -> your "why this is different" (mechanism).
- 10 exact phrases -> use them word for word in hooks and headlines.

Paste the output into the brief in [templates/ad-brief.md](templates/ad-brief.md).
