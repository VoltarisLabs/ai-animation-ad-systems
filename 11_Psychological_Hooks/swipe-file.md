# Swipe File: Annotated Example Ads

These are original training examples written for this playbook across different industries. They are not real campaigns and their numbers are placeholders. Each one is annotated so writers can see *why* it works. Add real winners from `test-log.md` at the bottom.

---

### 1. B2B SaaS, problem-aware, Meta static (PAS)
> **Image text:** Still chasing late invoices?
> **Primary:** You finished the work 45 days ago. You're still sending "just checking in" emails. [Tool] sends automatic, polite reminders and a one-click pay link, so clients pay in days, not weeks. Try it free for 14 days.
> **Headline:** Get paid in days, not weeks

**Why it works:** The hook is a pain in the customer's own words. "45 days" and "just checking in" are specific, recognizable details. The mechanism is named (automatic reminders + pay link). The first step is free and risk-free.

### 2. Local service (dentist), solution-aware, Google Search
> **Headlines:** Same-Week Dental Implants | Free 3D Scan and Consult | Rated 4.9 by 800+ Patients | Payment Plans From $99/mo
> **Description:** See your new smile on a 3D scan before you commit. Free consult, same-week appointments, and payment plans. Book online in 60 seconds.

**Why it works:** It answers what searchers want (speed, trust, cost). The price is broken into a monthly amount. "See it before you commit" lowers risk. The CTA sets expectations ("60 seconds").

### 3. Fitness app, unaware, 30s UGC (Star-Story-Solution)
> **0-3s:** "I used to think I was just lazy."
> **3-12s:** "I'd buy a gym plan in January and quit by February. Every year."
> **12-22s:** "Turns out the plans were 60 minutes long. I have 15. [App] builds 12-minute workouts around my actual schedule."
> **22-30s:** "I'm on week 11. If you're 'lazy' too, try the free week."

**Why it works:** The hook is about identity, not the product. It reframes the failure as not their fault. The mechanism (12 minutes) answers the real cause. "Week 11" is specific proof. The CTA repeats the viewer's own label.

### 4. Skincare e-commerce, product-aware, retargeting (objection-buster)
> **Hook text:** "Won't this break me out?"
> **Body:** It's the #1 question we get. Our formula has 0 fragrance and 0 essential oils, and it was tested on sensitive skin. If you react, we refund the full bottle. Even if it's empty.

**Why it works:** It raises the worry before the viewer can. The proof is specific. The guarantee is bold and removes the risk. Only people who already know the product see it, so it doesn't need to explain the product.

### 5. Online course, most aware, Stories (offer + deadline)
> "Doors close Friday. Enroll by midnight Thursday to get the 5 client-pitch templates free. [CTA]"

**Why it works:** Most-aware buyers need the offer only. The deadline is real and tied to a cohort start. The bonus removes the next objection ("but how do I get clients?").

### 6. Agency, product-aware, LinkedIn (us vs. them)
> **Intro:** Most agencies send you a report. We send you booked calls. Last quarter: 312 meetings for 9 B2B clients, with every call logged in your CRM. We take 3 new clients a month.

**Why it works:** The contrast is an output, not an activity. The numbers are specific. The capacity limit is a true form of scarcity and also frames them as the prize (Klaff).

### 7. Home services, problem-aware, TikTok (listicle)
> "3 signs your water heater is about to quit: 1) rusty water, 2) rumbling sounds, 3) it's over 10 years old. If you hit 2 of 3, get a free check before it floods your basement."

**Why it works:** It's useful on its own, so it earns attention even from non-buyers. It lets viewers self-diagnose (commitment). The loss framing is accurate (flooding). The first step is free.

### 8. Real estate (cash buyer), problem-aware, Meta (Housing category)
> **Primary:** Inherited a house you don't want to deal with? We buy as-is: no cleanouts, no repairs, no showings. You pick the closing date. Get a no-obligation cash offer.

**Why it works:** The situation call-out does the targeting, which matters because Housing-category ads can't target by demographics. It removes three kinds of effort at once. "You pick the date" gives the seller control, which lowers fear.
