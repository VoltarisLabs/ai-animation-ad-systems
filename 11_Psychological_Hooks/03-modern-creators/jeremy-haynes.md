# Jeremy Haynes

**Who:** Paid-ads and high-ticket funnel operator (runs an "inner circle" coaching offer). Figures below are his claims.
**Source:** "The New Rules of Meta Ads (2027)", youtube.com/watch?v=kZmPPS4512I (published 2026-09-26), read in full.
**Core idea:** Meta now makes every lead hyper-aware and hyper-sophisticated within a few scrolls, so you have to sell as if they've already seen every competitor.

## 1. Frameworks

### The dartboard
Meta guesses the one thing a person is most likely to buy right now. The moment they click or linger on it, Meta keeps serving similar ads from other advertisers for days, across Feed, Reels, Stories and its other apps, until that interest is exhausted. One click, and they are flooded with every competitor, price and pitch in the category.

### Everyone is now at Stage 4
With Schwartz's stages of sophistication (see [../01-copywriters/eugene-schwartz.md](../01-copywriters/eugene-schwartz.md)), the algorithm pushes most people to the last stage almost overnight. That means you need a **unique mechanism plus your whole process on display.**

### Back-end selling systems
Everything between the opt-in and the sale has to sell:
- The confirmation page teaches instead of just saying "thanks."
- The emails echo the same message.
- Setters are genuinely helpful and start the sale rather than just qualifying.
- What AI tools say about you matters.
- Content keeps showing up in their feed afterwards.

### Five views of how you stack up
1. Yours.
2. Your customers', which matter more than yours.
3. AI's: ask the models how you compare.
4. The algorithm's: use burner accounts to trigger your own ads and see who appears next to you.
5. Secret shopping your competitors.

## 2. Actionable rules
1. **Shorten the funnel cycle.** Every day between click and conversation is a day of competitor ads. Keep webinars within about 72 hours of the opt-in, book calls soon after the click, and start teaching immediately.
2. **Assume they've seen every alternative.** Sell against the alternatives and make the mechanism and process visible.
3. **"Hammer them":** add to the flood yourself with short and long content in front of people who opted in.
4. **Radical proof.** His example lists every completed deal with a link to its real financials and a video breakdown. He claims a 75% show rate and a 40% close rate on a $20K+ offer.
5. **Show the numbers buyers care about** (he shows his own dispute rate). Buyers with little money are most afraid of losing it, so make risk reversal as strong as you truthfully can.
6. **Talk to customers, especially the unhappy ones.** They tell you exactly how you compare. Don't default to charging less; premium can be the position.
7. **Be everywhere, with genuine value.** He says buyers nearly always touch several platforms before buying, so being where competitors aren't is an edge.

## 3. Psychological triggers
- **Comparison bias:** every lead is comparing you, so give them the comparison yourself (matches "Contrast").
- **Radical transparency / proof:** matches "Honest admission" and "Social proof."
- **Mere exposure through repeated, useful content:** matches "Cognitive ease."

## Apply it (example: local home-buyer ads)
- A seller who clicks will see every other "we buy houses" ad for days. Call or text back fast, and make the first reply teach: how an as-is sale works, and what they don't have to do.
- The first text and the landing page should say how you differ from the alternatives, using only confirmed terms.
- Build a proof page of real past purchases (photos, walkthrough, what the seller didn't have to do), with sellers' consent and real data only.
- Check what ChatGPT and Gemini say when asked about the business and about "cash home buyers in [city]." Use a burner account to see whose ads appear next to yours.
