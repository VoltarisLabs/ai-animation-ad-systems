## Alex Cattoni (Copy Posse)
**Focus:** Sales pages, email sequences, ad hooks, empathy-driven selling.

**Frameworks**
- **Emotional Buyer's Journey.** A buyer should feel five things in order: Understood -> Safe -> Empowered -> Excited -> Committed.
- **5 Ps.** Problem -> Pain -> Prescription -> Pivot (why you are qualified) -> Positioning (why yours is best). **Unverified**: not confirmed in a second source.
- Her sales-page formula taught in *Write & Ignite* is paywalled: **unverified**.

**Rules**
- Use three adverbs or fewer per piece. Avoid passive voice. Break up long sentences.
- Always give a reason to act now (the Committed stage).

**Triggers:** Feeling understood, safety and trust, empowerment, urgency.
**Sources:** amyporterfield.com/2024/08/706/ ; copyposse.com/ignite
**Confidence:** Medium (both frameworks come from one podcast recap).

## Joanna Wiebe (Copyhackers)
**Focus:** Conversion copywriting. Research-led, test-validated copy.

**Frameworks**
- **3-part conversion copywriting process:** (1) Research and discovery (most of the work), (2) Write, wireframe and edit, (3) Validate and experiment, for example with 5-second tests.
- **Review mining.** Take headlines and objections straight from customer language in reviews of your product or of competing and adjacent products. Sort quotes into loves, hates and worries.
- **Message hierarchy.** Rank the messages before you write the page.
- **Rule of One.** One reader, one offer, one promise, one big idea.
- **Seven Sweeps.** An editing method that starts with the Clarity sweep.
- She teaches Schwartz's awareness stages on landing pages. The top of the page should match the ad and the visitor's awareness stage, and the rest moves them forward.

**Rules**
- Don't invent the message. Find it in the customer's words.
- Message match: the landing page headline has to continue the ad's promise.

**Triggers:** Voice-of-customer language, message match, reducing anxiety.

**Teardown:** For a rehab center (Beachway), a headline taken from Amazon book reviews, "If you think you need rehab, you do," got 400%+ more clicks than the control. Form submissions on the next page rose about 20%.
**Sources:** copyhackers.com/2014/10/amazon-review-mining/ ; copyhackers.com/3-part-conversion-copywriting-process-for-newbies/ ; copyhackers.com/2022/06/what-is-conversion-copywriting/
**Confidence:** High.

## Neville Medhora (Kopywriting Kourse, SwipeFile)
**Focus:** Short, clear sales copy and email. Removes corporate fluff.

**Frameworks:** AIDA as his default structure. "Write Like You Talk." SwipeFile.com as a library of examples. He uses PAS but did not create it.

**Rules**
- Write the way you speak, in your own personality.
- If an idea takes more than 3 sentences to explain, it is too long. (**Unverified** in a primary source.)
- Cut buzzwords that make the reader stop and decode.
- Draft several headlines and pick the best one.

**Triggers:** Curiosity (Attention), stacked benefits (Desire), one clear CTA.
**Sources:** Kopy Checklist PDF; kopywriting.com/free-copywriting-course/
**Confidence:** Medium-high on AIDA / Write Like You Talk. Specific teardowns **unverified**.

## Kyle Milligan (Kyle the Writer, Copy Squad)
**Focus:** Long-form direct-response sales letters (financial and health).

**Frameworks**
- **NESB.** Every offer should feel **New, Easy, Safe and Big**. He calls these the four core buying emotions.
- "Beats of a Sales Letter" and "The Language of Copywriting": emotion carried by word choice. The detailed steps are paywalled: **unverified**.
- Book: *Take Their Money*.

**Rules:** Check every headline and lead against NESB. If one of the four is missing, add it. Write conversationally.
**Triggers:** Novelty, ease, safety/risk reversal, size of payoff.
**Claimed results (self-reported):** $7.1M and 51K customers from his copy in 2018. As copy chief at WealthPress (2019) he reportedly helped grow a small publisher into a $50M company.
**Sources:** Kyle Milligan LinkedIn NESB post; geniusesofcopywriting.com/kyle-milligan/ ; kylethewriter.com
**Confidence:** High on NESB.

## Roy Furr (Breakthrough Marketing Secrets)
**Focus:** Classic direct response applied to modern marketing. Campaign teardowns.

**Frameworks**
- **Only three Big Ideas.** Every selling message is one of these:
  1. **Problem:** an urgent problem you solve better than anyone.
  2. **Opportunity:** something new and 10x better.
  3. **Prediction:** a coming change you help people get through.
- **3-step story formula:** Setup -> Increasing conflict -> Resolution.
- **1% better every week** (he credits this to Gary Bencivenga).

**Rules:** Choose the Big Idea by temperature. Buyers already looking for a solution respond to Problem. Cold markets need demand created, so use Opportunity or Prediction.
**Triggers:** Urgency, novelty, anticipation or fear of change, narrative tension.
**Sources:** breakthroughmarketingsecrets.com/blog/three-big-ideas-selling-message/ ; /blog/the-3-step-story-formula/
**Confidence:** High.
