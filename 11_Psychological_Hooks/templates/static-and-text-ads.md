# Static, Carousel and Text Ad Templates

Character limits and sizes are in [../platform-specs.md](../platform-specs.md). Write to the visible length, not the maximum.

---

## Social static (Meta / LinkedIn / TikTok image)
**Image text (5 to 8 words max):** the hook or the result. Big, high contrast, readable on a phone.

**Primary text (the part people see before "See more" has to earn the click):**
```
Line 1: [Hook: pain, call-out or specific result]
Line 2: [Mechanism or proof in one line]
Line 3: [Offer + CTA]
--- (below the fold, optional) ---
[3 short bullets: benefit, benefit, objection answered]
[Guarantee or reason why]
[CTA repeated]
```
**Headline:** the result or offer, not the brand name. "Get [result] in [time]."
**Description (if shown):** proof or urgency. "Rated 4.9 by 1,200 [audience]."

### Static layouts that test well
1. **Big claim + product shot**
2. **Before / after** split
3. **Testimonial card:** quote, face, name, result
4. **Us vs. them** comparison table
5. **Stat / number** (one huge number + what it means)
6. **Notes app / text message** screenshot style
7. **"POV" or meme** format (only if it fits the brand voice)

## Carousel (3 to 7 cards)
| Card | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook. It must work alone and make them swipe |
| 2 | Problem or "most people do X" |
| 3-5 | One point, step or proof per card |
| Last | Offer + CTA |

## Google Search (Responsive Search Ads)
Write headlines that can be read in any combination.
- **Headlines (mix these types):** keyword match (x3), benefit (x3), proof/number (x2), offer (x2), CTA (x2), objection-buster (x2), brand (x1).
- **Descriptions:** (1) benefit + proof, (2) offer + CTA, (3) mechanism / why us, (4) objection answered + CTA.
- Pin only what must appear (e.g., a compliance line). Pinning reduces testing.
- Match search intent: someone searching "[service] near me" wants availability and trust, not education.

## Landing page hero (message match)
```
Headline:     [Same promise as the ad, in the same words]
Subhead:      [How: mechanism + time + no-effort]
Proof strip:  [logos / star rating / "1,200 customers"]
CTA button:   [First-person, specific: "Get my free quote"]
Under CTA:    [Risk reducer: "No credit card. Takes 30 seconds."]
```
