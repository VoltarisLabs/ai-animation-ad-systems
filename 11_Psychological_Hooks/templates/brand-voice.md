# Brand Voice Template

Fill in one copy per brand or client. Every writer and every AI prompt uses it so ads sound like one company.

```
Brand:                  [name]
We sound like:          [e.g., "a blunt friend who happens to be an expert"]
We never sound like:    [e.g., "a bank," "a hype bro," "a corporate press release"]

3 voice traits (with do / don't):
1. [Trait]   Do: "[example line]"   Don't: "[example line]"
2. [Trait]   Do: "[example line]"   Don't: "[example line]"
3. [Trait]   Do: "[example line]"   Don't: "[example line]"

Reading level:          [aim for grade 5-7 for ads]
Person:                 [we/you, or first person founder]
Words we use:           [customer phrases, product terms]
Words we ban:           [jargon, overused hype words, regulated terms]
Emoji / slang policy:   [yes / no / platform-dependent]
Claims we can make:     [with proof source]
Claims we can't make:   [legal or platform limits]
Sign-off / CTA style:   [e.g., "Get my quote" not "Submit"]
```

## Voice test
Read the ad aloud, then hide the logo. Could a customer tell it's your brand? If not, go back to the traits.
