# Static Ad Templates

For single-image and carousel ads on Meta (Feed, Stories, Reels placements) and TikTok. For layouts, start from `06_Canva_Ad_Templates/`.

## Static rules (every image)
1. **The 1-second test.** Someone scrolling fast should get the point in one second. That means one message and one focal point.
2. **8 words or fewer of image text.** The image carries the hook or the result. The primary text does the explaining.
3. **Readable at thumbnail size.** Shrink it to 25% on your screen. If you can't read the text, make it bigger or cut words.
4. **High contrast.** Dark text on light, or light on dark. No text over busy parts of a photo.
5. **Faces and eyes stop the scroll.** A real person looking at the camera or at the product. Emotion that matches the hook.
6. **Show the product or the result**, not an abstract stock photo.
7. **Logo small, in a corner.** The hook is the hero, not the brand.
8. **Build each size separately.** 4:5 for Feed and 9:16 for Stories and Reels, with text inside the safe zones listed in [video-scripts.md](video-scripts.md) rule 6. Don't just crop one into the other.
9. **Looks native.** Screenshot, note, text-message and UGC-photo styles often beat polished designs. Test both.
10. **Make 3 versions of every concept:** same image with 3 different hooks, or same hook on 3 different images. Change one thing only.

## Copy that goes with the image
**Primary text** (the first line is all most people read before "See more"):
```
Line 1: [Hook: pain, call-out or specific result. Front-load it; Feed truncates.]
Line 2: [Mechanism or proof in one line]
Line 3: [Offer + CTA]
--- (below the fold, optional) ---
[3 short bullets: benefit, benefit, objection answered]
[Guarantee or reason why]
[CTA repeated]
```
**Headline** (Feed, about 27 characters): the result or the offer, not the brand name. "Get [result] in [time]."

## Layouts that test well
Which layout for which awareness level: see the master table in [../AD_COPY_CHECKLIST.md](../AD_COPY_CHECKLIST.md) (Step 1).

| # | Layout | Image text template | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Big claim + product | "[Result] in [time]." | Solution-aware |
| 2 | Before / after split | "Before: [pain] / After: [result]" | Solution-aware (not for health, weight, beauty or income results) |
| 3 | Testimonial card | "'[Quote with a result]' [name, role]" | Product-aware |
| 4 | Us vs. them table | "[Them]: [flaw] / [Us]: [fix]" | Product-aware |
| 5 | One big number | "[Number] [what it means]" | Any, proof-led |
| 6 | Notes app / text message | "[Pain written like a real text]" | Unaware or problem-aware, native feel |
| 7 | Call-out | "[Audience], stop [mistake]." | Problem-aware |
| 8 | Question | "Still [pain]?" | Problem-aware |
| 9 | Offer card | "[Offer]. [Deadline]." | Most aware, retargeting |
| 10 | POV / meme | "POV: you just [relatable moment]" | Unaware (only if it fits the brand) |

## Carousel (3 to 7 cards)
| Card | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hook. It must work alone and make them swipe |
| 2 | Problem or "most people do X" |
| 3-5 | One point, step or proof per card |
| Last | Offer + CTA |

## Static QA before launch
- [ ] Passes the 1-second test.
- [ ] 8 words or fewer on the image, readable at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Text inside the safe zone for every size.
- [ ] Image text, primary text and headline all tell the same single promise.
- [ ] 3 variants built, each differing by one element.
- [ ] Passes [../AD_COPY_CHECKLIST.md](../AD_COPY_CHECKLIST.md).
