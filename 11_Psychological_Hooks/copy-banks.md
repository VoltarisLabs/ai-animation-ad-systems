# Copy Banks

Ready-to-adapt lines for the parts of a short-form video or static ad that aren't the hook. Hooks are in [hook-formulas.md](hook-formulas.md). The psychology behind each line is in [triggers-index.md](triggers-index.md).

`[brackets]` = fill in. Use only claims you can prove.

---

## 1. CTA bank
Write CTAs in the **first person** ("Get my quote," not "Get your quote") and make them **specific**. Pair the spoken or on-screen CTA with the closest platform button.

| Awareness | Spoken / on-screen CTA | Closest button |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | "See how it works." / "Watch the 60-second breakdown." | Learn More |
| Problem-aware | "Take the 30-second quiz." / "Get the free checklist." | Learn More, Download |
| Solution-aware | "See my price." / "Get my free [thing]." | Get Quote, Sign Up |
| Product-aware | "Start my free week." / "Compare plans." | Sign Up, Shop Now |
| Most aware | "Claim my spot by [day]." / "Book my [time] slot." | Book Now, Shop Now |

**CTA rules**
- One CTA per ad. It should match the button.
- Say what happens next and how long it takes: "Takes 30 seconds," "No card needed."
- A soft CTA (Learn More) often wins on cold audiences. A direct CTA (Book Now) often wins on warm ones. Test both.

## 2. Primary text: line-1 openers
The first line is often all anyone reads before "See more." It has to carry the hook alone.
1. "[Audience]: [specific result] without [thing they hate]."
2. "Still [pain]? Here's what's actually causing it."
3. "[Specific number] [people like them] did [X] last [month]."
4. "We tested [X] vs. [Y] for [T]. [Winner] won by [margin]."
5. "You don't need [common solution]. You need [mechanism]."
6. "[Situation] is costing you about [cost] a [time]."
7. "Here's the [number]-step way to [R], start to finish."
8. "'[Customer quote with a result].' [Name, role]"
9. "If you [situation], read this before you [common action]."
10. "[Offer], until [real date]. Here's why we're doing it."

## 3. Headline formulas (keep near 27 characters for Feed)
| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Get [result] in [time] | Get paid in 7 days |
| [Result], not [pain] | Calls, not reports |
| Free [thing] for [audience] | Free scan for new patients |
| The [adjective] way to [R] | The 12-minute workout |
| [Number] [audience] switched | 1,200 salons switched |
| [Result]. No [pain]. | Sold as-is. No repairs. |
| See your [thing] first | See your price first |
| Try it free for [T] | Try it free for 14 days |

## 4. Objection bank
Answer the biggest worry inside the ad, before they can scroll away with it.

| Objection | One-line answer pattern | Proof type |
|---|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "Less than [cheaper comparison] a [time]." | Price per day, cost of doing nothing |
| "No time." | "[X] minutes to start. We do the rest." | Setup time, done-for-you |
| "Won't work for me." | "Built for [their exact situation]." | A similar customer's result |
| "I've tried this before." | "Most [alternatives] fail because [cause]. We [mechanism]." | Mechanism, before/after of the process |
| "Is it legit?" | "[Credential]. [Years]. [Number] customers." | Reviews, licenses, faces |
| "What's the catch?" | "No catch. We [how you make money]." | Reason why |
| "I'll do it later." | "Every [time] you wait, [accurate cost]." | Loss framing |
| "Hard to switch." | "We move everything over for you." | Migration or setup included |
| "Locked in?" | "Cancel anytime, in one tap." | Terms shown |
| "Will I be pressured?" | "No calls unless you ask. No obligation." | Process shown |
| "Need to ask my partner." | "Send them this 60-second video." | Shareable proof |
| "Not sure it's for me." | "It's not for [wrong fit]. It is for [right fit]." | Honest admission |

## 5. Risky vs. safe (Meta and TikTok policy)
| Risky (often rejected) | Safe rewrite |
|---|---|
| "Are you overweight?" | "Workouts built for busy schedules." |
| "Struggling with debt?" | "A simple plan for tax season." |
| "Clear skin in 7 days." | "Gentle on sensitive skin." |
| Before/after body or face photos | Show the process or the product in use |
| "Make $10K a month from home." | "Learn the pitch we use with clients." |
| "Only 2 left!" (not true) | "We take [real number] clients a month." |
| "You won't believe..." | "[Specific benefit] in [specific time]." |
| "Lost your parent? Sell the house." | "Inherited a house you don't want to deal with?" |

The rule behind all of these: **name the situation, not the person**, and never promise health, body or income results.

## 6. Editing drills
**Readability:** aim for grade 5 to 6. Paste the copy into Hemingway Editor or any readability checker. Short words, short sentences.

**Spoken vs. written (for voiceover):** read it aloud at normal speed. Rewrite any line you stumble on, any line you'd never say out loud, and any sentence over about 15 words.

**Words to cut:** revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing, world-class, seamless, leverage, solutions, innovative, "we are proud to," "don't miss out," very, really, just, basically.

**Words that usually earn their place:** you, your, free, new, now, because, how, here's, finally, instantly (if true), a specific number, a specific place.

**Five rewrite drills** (do each one before writing a new ad):
| Before (vague) | After (specific) |
|---|---|
| "Fast, reliable service." | "On your roof within 48 hours. Or the inspection's free." |
| "Save money on your bills." | "The average customer cut $47 a month." |
| "High-quality ingredients." | "3 ingredients. No fillers. Made in Ohio." |
| "Trusted by many customers." | "Rated 4.9 by 1,200 local families." |
| "Easy to use." | "Set up in 11 minutes. No training." |

(Numbers in examples are placeholders. Use your own.)
