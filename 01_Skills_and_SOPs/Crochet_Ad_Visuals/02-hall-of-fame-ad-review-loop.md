## What this page is

Not a technical lesson. It's the **feedback loop step** — the course's position is that finishing production is not finishing the ad.

> *"You just finished the Crochet Style course, but it's not done. That's when the questions start: Is my hook strong enough? Are my visuals easy to follow? How are my transitions?"*

The argument: get other people's eyes on the ad **before the market gives its answer** (i.e. before spending ad budget to learn it doesn't work).

---

## The 3 actions

1. **Export the ad.** *"Done beats perfect."*
2. **Post it in the `👀 Ad Reviews` channel.** Ask **specific questions** to get specific answers.
3. **Learn from feedback** — on your own ad and on everyone else's posted below.

Reviewers: Camilo, the coaches, or any member who has already nailed it.

## How this applies to us

We already learned this lesson the expensive way. The **v6 rejection** happened because the ad went straight from production to the company owner with no intermediate review — and the failure causes (course-banned models, inverted G.E.M order, invented script instead of the client's approved one) were all things a reviewer would have caught before any credits were spent.

**Practical takeaway for our process:** before sending an ad to J bro, run it against the pre-flight checklist in `creativeAds2/README.md` and the methodology memory. That's our stand-in for the `👀 Ad Reviews` channel.

**Worth doing:** posting the Altura ad (`ALTURA_FINAL_with5BROLL.mp4`) in Ad Reviews with specific questions would give us outside eyes at zero credit cost — and the tier progression could return free credits, which is our actual bottleneck.

Specific questions worth asking if we post it:
- Does the 1.7x speed read as energetic or rushed?
- Do the 5 B-roll cut-ins land on the right words?
- Is the "3 signs" listicle hook strong enough for a cold financial audience?
